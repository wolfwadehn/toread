Auto Arrange

Behavior

When the palletizing view is opened, deposit sites for parts from various layouts may not be synchronized with each other (parts scattered/unsorted). Clicking Auto Arrange:

  • Groups parts into piles by part type.
  • Displays each pile's height via a shadow drawn near the pile — the taller the pile, the longer the shadow (magnitude controlled by the "Shadow Scale factor", see 06-deposit-settings.md).
  • Obeys the active Deposit Settings: margins, spacing between piles, and maximum pile height.

[Target] extension: auto-suggested stacking patterns

The concept paper raises open questions about the criteria for automatic arrangement beyond simple "one pile per part type" grouping — candidates explicitly named: minimal area usage, grouping by order/job, and (unresolved, per 11-requirements-and-traceability/02-open-questions-and-assumptions.md) others. It also asks for nesting-like auto-suggestion: "Idealerweise gibt es, wie beim Verschachteln, die Möglichkeit, die Stapel für das Absortieren automatisch zu generieren... Der Programmierer kann diese dann direkt übernehmen oder ggf. noch anpassen, ähnlich wie beim Schachteln" — ideally, as with nesting, stacks for sorting are auto-generated and the programmer can accept or adjust them, the same way nesting proposals work today.

Requirements for the [Target] auto-arrange

  1. Auto Arrange must additionally respect the constraints introduced in later chapters that MetaCAM's original version did not have:
    • Reachability zones (06-process-and-collision-constraints/02-reachability-and-non-reachable-zones.md).
    • Orientation-restricted zones (04-pallet-definition/04-pallet-zones-margins-and-orientation.md).
    • Excentric-gripper stack-height limits (03-gripper-definition/03-excentric-gripper-and-stack-height-limits.md).
  2. Result must remain fully editable afterward via the manual Pick tool (05-manual-pick-split-and-merge.md), exactly like a nesting proposal remains editable.
  3. The specific optimization objective (minimal area vs. grouped-by-order) is an open question — see 11-requirements-and-traceability/02-open-questions-and-assumptions.md. [V1] should default to "one pile per part type, filled in nesting/part order" (today's MetaCAM behavior) until the objective is decided.
