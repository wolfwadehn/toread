Mini-Nest and Frame Handling

[Target]

Use case

Small parts are often left joined to each other or to the skeleton by microjoints/nanojoints at the point of unloading, so a group of parts can be removed and processed together rather than individually. This "mini-nest" (or frame) is a cutting-side grouping decision (made in nesting, not in palletizing), but it has direct consequences for how this module builds and books piles.

Mini-nest as a single addressable unit

A mini-nest physically contains multiple part instances, but for stacking purposes it must be handled the same way a single part instance is handled elsewhere in this spec:

  • It occupies one deposit location and moves as one unit through Pick/Split/Merge (05-manual-pick-split-and-merge.md) — the same non-splittable-unit rule already documented there for a pile backed by a single NC deposit location applies here, except the "unit" is now a mini-nest rather than a single part.
  • It stacks as one Members entry in the pile (01-stack-data-model.md), not as N separate entries for its N constituent parts.
  • Its Stacking no. / Unload Sequence value is assigned once, for the mini-nest as a whole, matching how a single part instance is sequenced today (08-export-lst/02-populated-fields-reference.md).

Extending PartTypeRef

01-stack-data-model.md states that all members of one pile are the same part type, referenced by PartTypeRef. A mini-nest extends this without contradicting it: PartTypeRef may point to a mini-nest type (a fixed composition of N part instances joined by micro/nanojoints) instead of a single part type. All homogeneity rules that apply to PartTypeRef today (pile membership, Equalize/Lower Pile Height eligibility — 04-equalize-and-lower-pile-height.md) apply unchanged, treating two mini-nests as "the same type" only if they carry the identical part composition and joint layout.

Quantity tracking and booking

Because a mini-nest's constituent parts are not individually removable until the joints are broken downstream, TotalNumber on a mini-nest pile counts mini-nest units, not raw part instances. This has two consequences that must be surfaced to the operator and to downstream booking:

  1. Order/quantity booking must be able to resolve a mini-nest unit back into its N part-instance quantities — e.g. an order for 500 pieces of a part that is nested 5-up per mini-nest is satisfied by 100 mini-nest units, and booking/completion tracking must report against the part-level quantity the order was placed in, not the mini-nest count.
  2. TotalHeight / SheetStackHeight continue to use per-mini-nest thickness (the Stacking Thickness override, if the mini-nest's effective stacked height differs from a single sheet thickness — see 07-schedule-and-sheet-count.md), not the sum of its individual parts' thicknesses (they lie in-plane within the mini-nest, not stacked on top of each other).

Open point

Whether mini-nest composition is authored in nesting and merely carried through by this module, or whether the palletizing module needs its own UI to split/regroup a mini-nest before stacking, is not resolved by the source material and is out of scope for this chapter.
