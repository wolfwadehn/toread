Reachability and Non-Reachable Zones

The mechanical-limitation case (② in the backward-planning chain)

The concept paper's illustration shows a robot gripper reaching over a pallet, with a clearly marked "Non reachable area even" zone at the far pallet corner. Example given: a Stopa cart's position relative to the Bedmaster limits accessibility. The non-reachable area depends on:

  1. Position of the cart/pallet relative to the machine.
  2. Gripper geometry, particularly excentric offset (see 03-gripper-definition/02-gripper-geometry-and-reach-envelope.md).
  3. Current stack height (see 03-gripper-definition/03-excentric-gripper-and-stack-height-limits.md).

The visualization requirement (Fabian's summary)

"Bei der Programmierung der Palettierung/Entladen auf Lagerpalette soll dem Programmierer direkt transparent gemacht werden, welche Teile innerhalb des Greifbereichs des BendMaster abgelegt sind und welche Außerhalb."

Translation/requirement: when programming palletizing/unloading onto a storage pallet, the programmer must be shown directly and transparently which parts are deposited within the BendMaster's gripper reach and which are outside it. The accompanying diagrams show:

  • A top-down pallet layout split into a non-reachable band (top strip, shaded) and a reachable band (rest of the pallet), with example part footprints (L-shaped brackets, rectangles, octagons) drawn inside each band.
  • A second variant overlays a thumbs-up icon on stacks with no issue and a lightning-bolt icon on stacks in collision/out of reach, within the reachable band — i.e. being in the "reachable" band is necessary but not sufficient; a stack can still individually collide (see 04-collision-hazard-of-repositioned-parts.md).

Module requirements

  1. Render the non-reachable zone as a persistent background overlay on the pallet canvas (not just a one-time warning) — ties into 05-stack-and-pile-management/02-stack-status-visualization.md.
  2. Any pile whose footprint falls (even partially) in the non-reachable zone is flagged the same way a collision is flagged (red / lightning-bolt), reusing the existing stack-status color convention rather than inventing a new one.
  3. The non-reachable zone must be recomputed whenever: the bound automation/ gripper changes (02-select-automation/04-impact-of-automation-change.md), the pallet's Position changes (04-pallet-definition/01-pallet-data-model.md), or a pile's height crosses the gripper's derating threshold.

Restricted / keep-out zones [Target]

Distinct from the non-reachable zone above: a non-reachable zone is derived from gripper geometry and cart/pallet position (it moves if the gripper or position changes). A restricted/keep-out zone is a static safety or equipment-clearance rule — e.g. a fenced area, a fixed obstacle, or a clearance envelope around other automation equipment — that applies regardless of which gripper is bound. Collision-free stack formation must avoid both: piles must not be placed where the automation cannot physically reach (non-reachable zone) and must not be placed where doing so would bring the automation or the stack into conflict with a restricted area, even if that area is technically within reach. Keep-out zones are configured per site/cell, not recomputed from gripper geometry, and are rendered with the same persistent overlay mechanism as the non-reachable zone (requirement 1 above) so both are visible together on the pallet canvas.
