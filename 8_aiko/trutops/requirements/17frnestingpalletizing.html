FR — Nesting & Palletizing

Reverse-engineered requirement draft — derived from static analysis of legacy source, NOT an authoritative specification. Treat SHALL statements as reconstructed intent.

1. Purpose & Scope

This document specifies the functional requirements for the Nesting & Palletizing area of the TruTops CAD/CAM suite: the automatic and interactive arrangement (Schachteln = nesting) of sheet-metal parts onto raw sheets/tables (Tafeln), and the arrangement (Palettierung = palletizing) of finished parts, scrap and skeleton onto pallets and stacks for automated unloading.

The area covers: automatic true-shape nesting (via GSSL's third-party NestLib engine), automatic rectangular and tile nesting, nesting to order quantities, per-part placement constraints (rotation, mirror, grain direction, priority), part clustering / mini-nests, common-cut (GTS) and part-in-part nesting, sheet-utilization optimization, multi-sheet and multi-format nests, interactive part manipulation, remnant/offcut (Resttafel) management with raw-material stock deduction, nesting result and report generation, and automatic and interactive palletizing/stacking of good parts (Gutteil), scrap (Abfall) and residual grid (Restgitter).

Editions served. The functionality is exposed as the stand-alone ToPsNest application (Applications\ToPsNest, surface F_ToPsSchachteln, TNEST.OLB) and is embedded in the punching edition ToPs 300 and the laser-cutting edition ToPs 100. Palletizing and automated sheet-unloading are technology- and automation-dependent (implemented per technology in tc_las/las_tech/palettierung and tc_stz/stz_tech/palettierung, driving LiftMaster/SortMaster automation).

Primary evidence modules: tc_nest (nesting core, controllers, NestLib interface, results, profiles), tc_palettierung (palletizing), with supporting tc_bof gritx interactive masks (aNest*, aTcoPalett, taf_*, tnestappl), the Nest SQL schema, and ToPsNest ini configuration.

2. Capability Overview

  • Automatic nesting of parts drawn from orders (Aufträge) onto raw sheets, using either the true-shape NestLib engine, a fast rectangular nester, or a tile/matrix nester (Kacheln).
  • Order-driven quantities: actual / minimum / maximum quantities and priorities per part; tracks how many were nested.
  • Per-part placement constraints: rotation permission and step angle, free rotation, mirroring, grain/rolling direction (Walzrichtung), priority, common-tool capability.
  • Part clustering / mini-nests: pre-built repeated arrangements (auto vertical/horizontal/best, grid, rectangular matrix, staggered, overlap).
  • Common-cut / common-edge nesting (GTS) and part-in-part nesting (placing parts inside inner contours/holes of larger parts).
  • Utilization optimization: multi-trial optimizer with configurable trial sets, time limit and minimum-improvement threshold; last-sheet filling and flat-end recomputation.
  • Multi-sheet / multi-format nests: identical-sheet (master-plate) production, and sheet-format forecasting (best single format vs. best multi-format combination, IFM).
  • Gridless (stegfrei) vs. grid-production (Gitterfertigung) nesting modes with configurable web widths (Stege) and sheet border margins per raw material.
  • Named nesting parameter profiles with layered default/local/override values and priority ordering.
  • Interactive nesting: place parts by ID / count / area, distribute, centre, move, rotate, mirror, copy, delete, clipboard, snap; manage multiple sheets (create/copy/delete/sort/reorder), change sheet format, and re-nest (Nachschachteln).
  • Remnant/offcut management: create remnant-sheet geometry (*.trg), manage remnant sheets, deduct raw material from stock (Abbuchen) and use sheet-format variants from the warehouse.
  • Results & reporting: sheet layout files (*.taf), result files (*.erg), configurable text reports, protocol/log, and an XML nesting report.
  • Palletizing: automatic palletizing proposal and interactive stack editing for good parts, scrap and skeleton onto configured pallets, with stacking strategy, collision handling, and unloading sequence for LiftMaster/SortMaster automation.

4. Functional Requirements

FR-NEST-001 — Automatic true-shape nesting of order parts

  • Requirement: When the user starts free-geometry nesting for a job, the system shall automatically place the ordered part instances onto raw sheets using the true-shape NestLib engine, respecting each part's true contour geometry.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: True-shape nesting is the core value of the area and maximises material use for irregular parts.
  • Source: tc_nest/nestlib_interface/TcNestLib.h (GSSL NestLib wrapper), tc_nest/nest_frgeo/TcNestHandler.h, gritx masks aNestStartFrGeo/aNestFrGeoParameter (tc_bof/aNestAuftrag.gritx), asm command nest order.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-002 — Automatic rectangular nesting

  • Requirement: Where rectangular nesting is selected, the system shall nest rectangle-like parts using a fast rectangular nester driven by a selectable placement strategy (by area, X-length, Y-length, combined XY-length, or priority).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Fast, deterministic nesting for rectangular/blank parts avoids the cost of full true-shape computation.
  • Source: tc_nest/nest_rect/TcRectangularNestHandler.h (enum TeNestRectangleStrategy: Area/XLength/YLength/XYLength/Priority), TcRectangularNestConfiguration.h (TePreferredDirection), gritx masks aNestStartErwRecht/aNestErwRechtParam.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-003 — Tile / matrix nesting

  • Requirement: Where tile nesting (Kacheln) is selected, the system shall arrange a single part type in a regular row/column matrix across the sheet according to configured tile parameters.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Tiling gives predictable, dense layouts for single high-volume parts.
  • Source: gritx masks aNestStartKacheln/aNestKachelnParamater (tc_bof/aNestAuftrag.gritx), JobModel TcTilesParameter (TyTilesParameterRef).
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-004 — Nesting to order quantities and priority

  • Requirement: The system shall nest each part up to its ordered quantity honouring the minimum, actual and maximum quantities and the part priority, and shall record the number of instances actually nested.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Nesting exists to fulfil order demand; over/under-production must be controllable.
  • Source: Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN columns TEIL_IST, TEIL_MIN, TEIL_MAX, TEIL_PRIORITAET, TEIL_ANZ_GESCHACHELT; tc_nest/nest_frgeo/TcNestParameter.h (m_differentPrioritiesUsed, sort criteria); NestLib NERR_PART_MIN_QTY_*.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-005 — Per-part rotation and step angle

  • Requirement: The system shall allow each part to be nested at rotations permitted by its rotation setting, using either a configured angular step increment or free rotation.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Controlled rotation is fundamental to achieving dense layouts while respecting part orientation rules.
  • Source: Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN TEIL_DREHEN, TEIL_DREHWINKEL_INK; nest profile params e_Param_PartStepAngle, e_Param_StepAngleMinimum, e_Param_PartFreeRotation (tc_nest/nest_profile/TcNestProfile.h).
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-006 — Per-part mirroring

  • Requirement: Where a part is flagged as mirrorable, the system shall permit its mirrored orientation to be used during nesting.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Mirroring doubles the placement options for asymmetric parts where the part geometry allows it.
  • Source: Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN TEIL_SPIEGELN; nest profile param e_Param_PartMirror; nesting frfl mirror source tc_nest/nest_frfl/frspiegl.h.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-007 — Grain / rolling-direction constraint

  • Requirement: While a grain (rolling) direction is defined, the system shall constrain the orientation of grain-sensitive parts to the sheet's grain axis (none, X, or Y).
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Brushed/grained material requires parts to keep a fixed orientation relative to the sheet texture.
  • Source: tc_nest/NestModel/JobModel/TcGrainDirection.hh (None/X/Y), Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN TEIL_WALZRICHTUNG_XY, nest profile params e_Param_GrainDirection/e_Param_PartGrainDirection, TcNestParameter::getGrainDirection.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-008 — Part clustering / mini-nests

  • Requirement: The system shall support nesting parts as clusters (mini-nests) built by a selectable cluster type, including automatic vertical/horizontal/best, user-defined, rectangular matrix, grid, staggered and overlap arrangements.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Clusters improve density and downstream handling for repeated small parts.
  • Source: tc_nest/nestlib_interface/TcNestLib.h NCLUSTER_* constants; JobModel TcPartCluster/TcPartClusterElement, tc_nest/NestController/TcPartClusterController.h; Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN TEIL_MINI_NEST/TEIL_MINI_NEST_NAME; profile params e_Param_ClusterFirst/e_Param_ClusterCutoffUtilization.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-009 — Common-cut / common-edge nesting (GTS)

  • Requirement: Where common-cut nesting (GTS) is enabled for GTS-capable parts, the system shall place parts so they share separating cuts, honouring the configured common-edge clearance and minimum common-edge length.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Shared cuts reduce cutting length/time and increase yield; only valid for GTS-capable parts and machines.
  • Source: nest profile params e_Param_CommonCut, e_Param_CommonEdgeProximity, e_Param_MinimumLengthCommonEdge, e_Param_PartCommonTool; TcNestLib.h COMMONEDGE/COMMONPUNCH; Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN TEIL_GTS_TAUGLICH, TEIL_GTS_X/Y, TEIL_GTS_BLOCK_X/Y.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-010 — Part-in-part nesting (nesting into inner contours)

  • Requirement: Where part-in-part / inner-contour filling is enabled, the system shall nest smaller parts inside the unused inner contours (holes/islands) of larger nested parts.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Filling internal waste areas materially increases sheet utilization.
  • Source: nest profile params e_Param_UseInnerContours, e_Param_UseInnerContoursFirst, e_Param_PrefIsleFill; TcNestLib.h PIP flags NEST_PIP_OFF/ON/MULTI_TORCH; TcNestParameter m_mitOhneInnenFlaeche, m_innenFlaecheZuerst.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-011 — Web spacing and sheet border margins

  • Requirement: The system shall enforce configured web widths (Stege) between nested parts and configured top/bottom/left/right border margins to the sheet edge, using values held per raw material.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Minimum spacing and clamping-free border zones are required for cutting quality and machine handling.
  • Source: Common.MAT_SHEET_NEST_BORDERS (MSNB_WEBWIDTHX/Y, MSNB_TOP/BOTTOM/LEFT/RIGHTBORDER, MSNB_FREEFORMBORDER); TcNestParameter m_stegX/m_stegY, getMarginForParts; profile params e_Param_ClearanceX/Y.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-012 — Gridless vs. grid-production nesting mode

  • Requirement: The system shall support both gridless (stegfrei) nesting and grid-production (Gitterfertigung) nesting, and in gridless mode shall honour the configured minimum distance between a part and adjacent processing.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Gridless nesting suits laser cutting without micro-joints; grid production retains a supporting skeleton.
  • Source: TcNestParameter::isNestingModeGridless, m_gridlessModePartToToolDistance; gritx aNestAllg.gritx asm commands set stegfrei / set gitterfertigung.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-013 — Sheet-utilization optimizer

  • Requirement: Where the optimizer is enabled, the system shall run multiple nesting trials to maximise sheet utilization, honouring the configured trial sets, time limit, and minimum-improvement-utilization threshold.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Automated multi-trial optimization is what produces production-quality yield without manual iteration.
  • Source: TcNestParameter (TeFrgeoOptimizerMth, isOptimizerEnabled, setTimeLimitSeconds, finishOptimizerIfOpened); profile params e_Param_EnableOptimizer, e_Param_OptimizerTimeLimit, e_Param_EnableGridOptimizer, e_Param_MinimumImprovementUtilization, e_Param_EnableTurboMode; TcNestLib.h OPT_*_TRIALSET.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-014 — Identical sheets / master plates

  • Requirement: Where master-plate (identical-sheet) production is requested, the system shall generate repeated identical sheet layouts and shall only accept a master plate whose utilization meets the configured minimum/optimum utilization.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Identical layouts simplify high-volume production and machine setup.
  • Source: TcNestParameter m_identischeTafeln, m_masterPlateType; profile params e_Param_IdenticalSheets, e_Param_MasterPlateType, e_Param_MasterPlateMinimumUtilization, e_Param_MasterPlateOptimumUtil, e_Param_MasterPlateHonorPriority.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-015 — Multi-format sheet forecasting

  • Requirement: The system shall support selecting the sheet format either in a fixed order, as the best single sheet format, or as the best multi-format sheet combination (IFM forecaster).
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Choosing the optimal stock format/combination minimises waste across the job.
  • Source: TcNestParameter::setTypeOfForecasterIFM (0=order, 1=optimal single format, 2=multi-format); TcNestLib.h PROGRESS_IFM_*; Nest.TAFEL_VARIANT (VAR_ABMESS_X/Y/Z).
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-016 — Last-sheet filling and flat-end recomputation

  • Requirement: Where last-sheet handling is enabled, the system shall fill the final (partially used) sheet with additional filler parts and shall recompute the last sheet when it falls below the configured flat-end utilization limit.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Reduces waste on the trailing sheet of a job.
  • Source: TcNestParameter m_letzteTafel, m_flatEndAn, m_flatEnd, m_fuellTeileNurBisAufHoehe, m_nestFillerWithMainQty; profile params e_Param_LastSheet, e_Param_EnableFlatEnd, e_Param_FlatEnd, e_Param_FillToHeight.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-017 — Named, layered nesting parameter profiles

  • Requirement: The system shall store nesting parameters as named profiles with layered default, local and override values, and shall apply profiles in the order given by their configured priority when several are active.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Reusable profiles let planners standardise machine/material-specific nesting behaviour.
  • Source: tc_nest/nest_profile/TcNestProfile.h/TcNestProfileManager.h, tc_nest/nest_data/TcXMLNestingProfile.h, ini key TOPS_NEST_PROFILE_PATH=$(TOPS_DATA_PATH_SHARED)/NestingProfiles; TcNestParameter::applyProfileParameters, getBatchSortKey; profile param e_Param_BatchSortKey.
  • Verification: Inspection

FR-NEST-018 — Interactive part placement

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to interactively place parts onto a sheet by picking a part, specifying a quantity, placing within a defined area, distributing parts, and centring parts horizontally or vertically.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Manual placement is required to correct or supplement automatic nesting.
  • Source: gritx masks aNestInteraktPlacePartByID, aNestPlatzierenAnzahl, aNestVorschauBereich, tNestPlatzierenBereich/tNestPlatzierenTafel, tNestTeileVerteilenFrei*/tNestTeileVerteilenIntern*, tNestTeileZentrierenH/V (tc_bof/aNestVorschauwerkzeuge.gritx, aNestTafel.gritx); asm intakt_nest start.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-NEST-019 — Interactive part manipulation

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to move (by two points, horizontally, vertically, or by a distance), rotate (about its centre, about a picked point, by two points, or by an increment), mirror, copy, delete and clipboard cut/copy/paste selected nested parts.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Full interactive editing of the layout is core to interactive nesting.
  • Source: gritx masks tNestTeilBewege*, tNestTeilDrehe*/tNestTeilDrehenMitte, tNestTeilAbstand*, aNestVorschauMirror, aNestTeilKopieAnzahl, aNestLoescheTeil, aNestZwischenablageKopieren/Schneiden/Einfuegen (aNestTafel.gritx, aNestVorschauwerkzeuge.gritx); asm taf_modify part, taf_modify_setup rotate.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-NEST-020 — Re-nesting of parts (Nachschachteln)

  • Requirement: When the user requests re-nesting, the system shall re-nest the selected or already-nested parts onto the current sheet set using the active nesting method.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Re-nesting lets planners iterate after edits without rebuilding the whole job.
  • Source: gritx masks aNestNachschachtelnStart, aNestNachschachtelnFrGeo, aNestNachSchachtelnErwRecht, aNestAuftragRenestSelected (tc_bof/aNestAuftrag.gritx).
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-021 — Multi-sheet management

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user manage the sheets of a job by creating a new sheet, copying a sheet (with a specified count), deleting a sheet, sorting sheets by size ascending/descending, and reordering a sheet (forward, back, to begin, to end).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: A nesting job spans many sheets that must be organised and sequenced for production.
  • Source: gritx masks aNestNeueTafel, tNestKopiereTafel/aNestKopieAnzahl, aNestLoescheTafel, aNestSortSheetsSizeAsc/Desc, aNestMoveSheetForward/Back/Begin/End, order-level aNestAuftragErste/Naechste/Vorherige/LetzteTafel (tc_bof/aNestTafel.gritx, aNestAuftrag.gritx); asm tafel number, sheet create.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-NEST-022 — Sheet format change

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to change the format of an existing nested sheet and to insert a sheet of a new format selected from the available stock formats.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Planners must be able to switch to a better-fitting stock size after seeing the layout.
  • Source: gritx masks aNestAendereTafelformat, aNestNeueTafel, aNestNeuTafel (aNestTafel.gritx, aNestAllg.gritx); Nest.TAFEL_VARIANT (VAR_ABMESS_X/Y, VAR_GENUTZTE_SCHACHTELART).
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-023 — Remnant / offcut sheet creation and management

  • Requirement: When a nested sheet leaves a usable offcut, the system shall generate a remnant-sheet geometry (*.trg) from the unused sheet area, and shall allow that remnant to be managed and re-nested as a raw sheet.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Remnant reuse is a primary lever for reducing raw-material cost.
  • Source: tc_nest/nest_util/TcTafelRestGeo.h (creates/loads *.trg remnant geometry, writeTrgFile), gritx masks aNestErzeugeResttafel, aNestResttafelVerwaltung (aNestTafel.gritx, aNestAllg.gritx); asm tafel resttaf.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-024 — Raw-material stock and warehouse sheet variants

  • Requirement: The system shall read raw sheets and their format variants from the material database and shall deduct (abbuchen) consumed sheets from stock when a nesting result is booked.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Nesting must draw from and update real stock so material planning stays consistent.
  • Source: tc_nest/NestController/TcRawMaterialController.h (mat_rawmaterial, mat_sheet, raw sheets read from DB since TruTops 4.0), gritx aNestAbbuchenResttafel/aNestAbbuchenResttafelErwRecht with asm nest_rawmaterial deduct; Nest.TAFEL_VARIANT VAR_LAGERGUTID, VAR_BENUTZT_VON_AUFTRAG, VAR_EINTRAG_GESPERRT.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-025 — Utilization and waste display

  • Requirement: The system shall display, on request, the sheet utilization and the waste/offcut (Verschnitt) of the current nesting layout.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Immediate feedback on yield lets the planner judge layout quality.
  • Source: gritx masks tNestZeigeVerschnitt, aNestZeigeErgebnis (tc_bof/aNestAllg.gritx); TcNestParameter utilization thresholds; tc_nest/nest_result/nerg_taf.h.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-NEST-026 — Nesting result and sheet-layout files with configurable reports

  • Requirement: On completing a nesting run, the system shall write the sheet-layout files (*.taf) and a nesting result file (*.erg), and shall produce text reports whose order/part/sheet/summary/remnant lines are configurable.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Downstream NC generation and shop-floor documentation depend on these result artefacts.
  • Source: tc_nest/nest_result/nerg_taf.h (writes *.taf and *.erg), nerg_ord.h/nerg_prt.h/nerg_tfl.h; ini report format keys NEST_ERG_ORDER, NEST_ERG_PART, NEST_ERG_SHEET, NEST_ERG_SUM, NEST_ERG_RS; gritx aNestZeigeProtokoll, asm logging liste.
  • Verification: Inspection

FR-NEST-027 — XML nesting report

  • Requirement: The system shall generate a structured XML report of the nesting result with localizable text using the supplied translations and stylesheets.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: A machine-readable report supports integration and printing.
  • Source: tc_nest/XMLReport/ (NestResultTranslations, Stylesheets/nestresult-translations.xml, XMLReportProxy), tc_nest/NestCore/.
  • Verification: Inspection

FR-NEST-028 — Machine selection at nesting time

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to select or change the target machine for the nesting job, and shall re-evaluate the layout against the newly selected machine.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Nesting parameters, sheet sizes and clamping depend on the specific machine.
  • Source: gritx masks aNestChangeMachine, aNestChangeMachineAndRedraw (tc_bof/aNestAllg.gritx); Nest.MASCHINE/Nest.MAS_* SQL tables; TcMaschinenSchluessel in controllers.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-029 — Order export/import and sheet DXF export

  • Requirement: The system shall allow a nesting order to be exported and imported, and shall allow an individual nested sheet to be exported as a DXF file.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Interchange of nesting orders and sheet geometry supports external tooling and archiving.
  • Source: gritx masks aNestExportAuftrag, aNestImportAuftrag, aNestSpeichernTafelDXF (tc_bof/aNestAllg.gritx); tc_nest/taf_util/TcExportImportOrder.cpp; asm taf_xfer export.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-030 — Sample / master parts

  • Requirement: Where a part is flagged as a sample part (Musterteil), the system shall place a defined number of sample instances (or one of each) ahead of quantity-driven nesting.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Sample parts let planners validate a layout before committing full quantities.
  • Source: Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN TEIL_MUSTERTEIL; gritx masks aNestMusterteilAnzahl, aNestMusterteilJedes, tNestAnzeigenMusterteil; asm result sample.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-031 — Automatic palletizing proposal

  • Requirement: Where palletizing is licensed for the machine/automation, the system shall automatically propose stacks for the finished good parts, scrap and residual grid, honouring the configured stack-good arrangement (mixed or separated), stack growth (sequential or even), collision-avoidance strategy, unload sequence, and good-part handling (mixed, sorted, or fixed position).
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Automated palletizing is required for lights-out unloading on TRUMPF automation (LiftMaster/SortMaster).
  • Source: tc_palettierung/palettierung_impl/TcPalAutomatikKonfiguration.h (enums TeAkStgAnordnung, TeAkStWachstum, TeAkKollBehandlung, TeAkAbstapelReihenfolge, TeAkStgGutteil); gritx aTcoPalett.gritx asm pal_palettierung auto; stack goods TcPalGutteil/TcPalAbfall/TcPalRestgitter.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-032 — Pallet definition and stacking constraints

  • Requirement: The system shall respect the defined pallets when palletizing, including pallet size and offset, fixed-position pallets, maximum stack height, maximum allowed weight (when weight checking is enabled), under-table usage rule, and mix-container capability.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Physical pallet limits must be honoured to produce executable unload plans.
  • Source: Laser.SORT_PALETTE/Punch.SORT_PALETTE (SORT_PAL_GROESSE_X/Y, SORT_PAL_OFFSET_X/Y, SORT_PAL_FIXIERT/SORT_PAL_FIXPOS_X/Y, SORT_PAL_MAX_HOEHE, SORT_PAL_MAX_ZUL_GEWICHT, SORT_PAL_GEWICHT_PRUEFEN, SORT_PAL_UNTER_TISCH, SORT_PAL_MIXBEHAELTER); tc_palettierung/palettierung_impl/TcPalPalette.h; Laser.PALETTENTYP.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-033 — Interactive palletizing (stack editing)

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to interactively edit palletizing by creating, dividing, merging and eliminating stacks, moving a stack absolutely or incrementally, rotating a stack, unstacking, and querying stack / stack-good information.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Operators must be able to correct the automatic palletizing proposal.
  • Source: gritx tc_bof/aTcoPalett.gritx masks aTcoDivideStapel, aTcoMergeStapel, aTcoEleminateStapel, aTcoMoveStapelAbsolute/Incrementell, aTcoRotateStapel, aTcoAbstapeln, aTcoInfoStapel/aTcoInfoStapelgut; asm pal_palettierung pal_stapel/pal_stapelgut.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-NEST-034 — Stack positioning variants and start corner

  • Requirement: The system shall build stacks on a pallet according to the configured start corner and growth direction, supporting X-before-Y and Y-before-X positioning from each of the four corners.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Deterministic stack layout is needed to match the automation's placement kinematics.
  • Source: tc_palettierung/palettierung_impl/TcPalStapelPositionierung* classes (XvorY/YvorX × Lio/Liu/Reo/Reu), TcPalAutomatikKonfiguration enums TeAkStartecke, TeAkStapelFolge, TeAkPalettenFolge.
  • Verification: Test

FR-NEST-035 — Sheet-unload gripper handling for thin-sheet remnant separation

  • Requirement: Where thin-sheet remnant separation (AL16) is configured, the system shall reserve/avoid the unload gripper positions when nesting so parts keep a minimum clearance from the sheet-unload grippers.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Grippers that separate the residual grid on thin sheets must not collide with nested parts.
  • Source: TcNestParameter::getSheetUnloadGrippers, m_minDistFromSheetUnloadGrippers, getSheetUnloadGripperCutterDiameter; JobModel TcRemainderSheetUnloadGripper; tc_nest/NestController/TcSheetUnloadLiftController.h; gritx tNestDarstellungLiftmaster.
  • Verification: Test

5. External Interfaces (area-specific)

See [[30 - External Interface Requirements]] for full specifications.

  • GSSL NestLib — third-party true-shape nesting/optimization engine, wrapped by tc_nest/nestlib_interface/TcNestLib.h/TlNest.h and the newer NestXidl/NestCoreApi layer (tc_nest/nest_xidl, tc_nest/NestCore). Enforces TRUMPF calling/linkage conventions.
  • SQL Server database — Nest schema: TEILE_ERFASSEN (order parts + nesting attributes), TAFEL_VARIANT (stock sheet formats/variants), REGEL_ALLG (rules), MASCHINE/MAS_*; Common.MAT_SHEET_NEST_BORDERS (per-material web widths & border margins); Laser/Punch SORT_PALETTE and PALETTENTYP (palletizing).
  • File formats: *.taf (nested sheet layout), *.erg (nesting result/report), *.trg (remnant-sheet geometry), part geometry drawings; DXF export of sheets; XML nesting report (tc_nest/XMLReport). See [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]].
  • Machines & automation: TRUMPF punching (ToPs 300) and laser (ToPs 100) machines; LiftMaster/SortMaster/TrumaGrip loading, unloading and sorting automation (sheet-load strategies, grippers, disposal zones, double-wagon). See [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]].
  • Configuration: nesting profiles under $(TOPS_DATA_PATH_SHARED)/NestingProfiles; ToPsNest edition ini (TOPS_APP_PATH, TNEST.OLB, NEST_ERG_* report formats).

6. Assumptions & Constraints

See [[50 - Constraints & Assumptions]] for cross-cutting items.

  • True-shape nesting and optimization depend on the licensed GSSL NestLib engine; behaviour and error codes (NERR_*) are constrained by that library's version. (partly inferred)
  • Palletizing is not universally available: it is technology-specific (implemented under tc_las/las_tech/palettierung and tc_stz/stz_tech/palettierung) and requires suitable automation; the palletizing kernel originated from a ToPs 600 (bending) implementation and was generalised.
  • Nesting always operates within one machine/material context; changing the machine may invalidate the current layout.
  • Interactive commands are delivered through the GRIT mask framework and the MMK/asm_call interpreter; the exact backend semantics of some asm_call verbs are inferred from mask context. See [[22 - FR - UI Framework, Dialogs & Interaction (GRIT)]].
  • Raw sheets are read from the database (since TruTops 4.0) rather than from stored job defaults; some raw-sheet default attributes are held outside the persisted job (TsRawSheetAttributes).

7. Open Questions & Ambiguities

  • The precise semantics and full parameter set of the tile nester (Kacheln, TcTilesParameter) could not be fully determined; only the mask/parameter entry points were observed.
  • The exact meaning of some Nest.TEILE_ERFASSEN fields (TEIL_LEGE_METHODE, TEIL_AUTO_MTL_METHODE, TEIL_CUTTER, TEIL_STANZ_LASER) is inferred from names; their allowed value ranges were not confirmed.
  • The difference between the classic taf_*/taf_asm batch-order ("Tafel") workflow and the newer NestCore/JobModel/Xidl pipeline (which appears to be the current C#-backed engine) is only partially mapped; both appear to coexist in the snapshot.
  • tc_optimizer (with sub-modules opti_thermal, opti_expense, opti_meander, opti_chess, opti_tube) and tc_bsort/tc_fsel were listed as evidence modules but appear to concern cutting-sequence / thermal / batch-sort optimization rather than part-on-sheet nesting; they likely belong to [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]] and are noted here rather than documented.
  • Weight-based palletizing limits (SORT_PAL_MAX_ZUL_GEWICHT, SORT_PAL_GEWICHT_PRUEFEN) exist in the schema, but the source of per-part weight used for the check was not traced.
  • The relationship between Nest.SORT_PALETTE handling and the tc_palettierung core (which references DBSortPalette/DBSortEinheit) versus the standalone bending palletizing was not fully resolved.
  • Whether MiniNest/cluster definitions are shared with, or distinct from, the punching/laser common-cut logic in tc_stz/tc_las was not confirmed.
