FR — CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing

Reverse-engineered requirement draft — derived from static analysis of legacy source, NOT an authoritative specification. Treat SHALL statements as reconstructed intent.

[!note] This is the system-level summary of the CAD editing area. A command- and algorithm-level detailed specification now exists as the child sub-suite 10.1–10.8 (see Detailed Specification below). Read this document for grouped capabilities and scope; consult the detail documents for the exact behaviour, parameters, defaults, tolerances and source traceability of each individual command/algorithm.

Detailed Specification (command & algorithm level)

The requirements in this area are organised in two tiers. This document groups the editor into 35 system-level requirements (FR-CAD-001..035) suitable for scoping, review and coarse traceability. The detail sub-suite 10.1–10.8 refines that summary to the command/algorithm level: it gives every interactive GRIT mask and every supporting geometry-kernel command its own verifiable SHALL requirement, traced to its asm_call string and its TcDbase*Command / kernel source, and documents modes, parameters, unit-specific defaults, tolerances and degenerate-case behaviour. Each detail requirement carries its own ID prefix and refines one or more of the parent FR-CAD requirements above.

All eight detail documents (10.1–10.8) are specified, shown above with their ID prefix and requirement count. Together they refine the 35 system-level requirements into 457 command/algorithm-level requirements.

1. Purpose & Scope

This document specifies the functional requirements of the interactive 2D CAD drawing/geometry editor and its underlying geometry kernel in the TruTops suite. The area covers creation and editing of geometry primitives (points, lines, arcs, circles, rectangles, text), contours and layers, dimensioning, geometric transformations (move/rotate/mirror/scale), corner processing (trimming, filleting, chamfering, notching), equidistant/offset generation, boolean contour arithmetic (union/intersection/subtraction/hull/sweep), parametric variants and variables, geometry approximation (curve→line/arc segments), and geometry comparison (difference detection and Fourier-based contour similarity).

The CAD editor is the core application of the ToPsCad edition and is embedded as the drawing/geometry-editing front end of the fabrication editions ToPs 300 (punching, see [[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]]), ToPs 100 (laser, see [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]]) and ToPs 600 (bending, see [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]]). Several behaviours are gated by edition (e.g. "Sonderoption für ToPs 400", "NoToPs600" mask variants in tc_bof/cad_assi.gritx).

Evidence base: tc_cad (interactive command lexer / cad_asm), tc_dbase (geometry element database and command objects TcDbase*Command), GeoAlgorithmsKernel/GeoKernel (C# geometry kernel), tc_geoUtils, tc_geoPlan, tc_math, tc_aprox (approximation), tc_fou (Fourier descriptors), tc_poly, tc_profil, tc_contourArithmeticTool (boolean arithmetic), tc_vgl (comparison), and the GRIT interaction masks in tc_bof/aCadFunc.gritx, aCadCreateFunc.gritx, aCadDimFunc.gritx, cad_base.gritx, cad_assi.gritx, cad_var.gritx, tec_cad.gritx.

Cross-cutting definitions live in [[04 - Domain Model & Glossary]]; file-format concerns in [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]]; rendering/plotting in [[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]]; dialog/interaction mechanics in [[22 - FR - UI Framework, Dialogs & Interaction (GRIT)]].

2. Capability Overview

  • Primitive creation — points, lines (2-point, angle, horizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular, tangent, polygon), circles (centre+radius/diameter, 3-point, 2-tangent, concentric), arcs (3-point, centre-begin-end, centre-radius-angle, concentric, smooth/tangent-continuous), rectangles with configurable corners, and TrueType text.
  • Parametric macros — single hole, oblong/slot (Langloch), hole row (Lochreihe), hole circle (Lochkreis), hole grid (Lochgitter), round/square/rectangular/elliptical/tool-shaped and körner (centre-punch) patterns.
  • Editing & transformation — move, rotate, mirror, scale (by two points or about a centre), copy, stretch (Dehnen), extend/trim to element, split and merge elements, delete elements/contours/small features/double holes.
  • Corner & edge processing — fillet (Rundung), chamfer (Fase), notch (Klinkung), relief groove (Freistich), equidistant/offset contour (Äquidistante).
  • Contour operations — close open contours, melt/merge overlapping contours, mark/select contours, smooth/thin (element reduction), boolean arithmetic (union/intersection/subtraction), convex hull, sweep (Minkowski-style dilation), clip.
  • Dimensioning — linear (horizontal/vertical/aligned/free), angular, radial, diameter, arc-length, ordinate, with editable text, tolerances, format and defaults.
  • Attributes & layers — per-element line colour and line style, applied to single elements, selections or all elements; DXF layers mapped to colour/style on import.
  • Snapping (Fangen) — configurable snap mode (what), snap kind (how), snap region (where), snap radius, catch on elements / equidistants / auxiliary geometry.
  • Approximation — approximate a parametrised 2D curve (incl. splines) by line and arc segments under strategy control (tc_aprox).
  • Comparison — difference detection between a reference and a comparison drawing with colour highlighting (geo_diff, tc_vgl); Fourier-based contour similarity/relationship detection (tc_fou).
  • Parametric variants — named variables/expressions, references to geometry elements/bendings, parametric changes (move/rotate/mirror/delete/stretch/change radius/change bend angle/set property), loops and conditions, evaluated through an embedded Python interpreter.
  • Sheet & part utilities — extract individual parts from a sheet drawing, defoiling/foil removal, drawing scaling, undo/redo.

4. Functional Requirements

FR-CAD-001 — Create basic geometry primitives

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to create points, lines, circles and arcs, each with multiple construction methods (line: two-point, by angle, horizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular, tangent-to-element, tangent-to-tangent, polygon; circle: centre+radius, centre+diameter, three-point, two-tangent+point, concentric; arc: three-point, centre-begin-end, centre-radius-angle, concentric, diameter).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Primitive creation is the irreducible core of a 2D CAD editor.
  • Source: tc_bof/aCadCreateFunc.gritx masks aCadCreatePoint, aCadCreateLine2Points, aCadCreateLineAngle, aCadCreateLineParallel, aCadCreateLinePerpendicular, aCadCreateLineTangentPoint, aCadCreateLineTangentTangent, aCadCreateLinePolygon, aCadCreateCircleCenterRadius, aCadCreateCircle3Points, aCadCreateCircle2TangentPoint, aCadCreateCircleConcentric, aCadCreateArc3Points, aCadCreateArcCenterBeginEnd, aCadCreateArcCenterRadiusAngle, aCadCreateArcConcentric; tc_cad/cad_asm/{line,circle,arc,point}.{hpp,cpp}; command objects tc_dbase/dbase_command/TcDbaseCircleCommand.h.
  • Verification: Test — create each primitive by each method and confirm resulting geometry matches the construction inputs.

FR-CAD-002 — Create rectangles with configurable corners

  • Requirement: When the user creates a rectangle, the system shall allow each corner to be independently configured as a sharp corner, fillet (Rundung), chamfer (Fase) or notch (Klinkung), with corner parameters and optional tab (Lasche) definitions.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Rectangular blanks with processed corners are the most common sheet-metal base shape; the rectangle assistant is a dedicated dialog tree.
  • Source: tc_bof/aCadCreateFunc.gritx aCadCreateRectangle, aCadCreateChamferRectangle, aCadCreateChamferSquare; tc_bof/cad_assi.gritx rectangle-assistant masks (M_CadRechteckAssistent, F_CadRechteckZeileEckeSetFase, ...SetKlink, ...SetRund, F_CadRechteckZeileLasche*); tc_cad/cad_asm/rect.{hpp,cpp}; tc_dbase/dbase_cad/TcDbaseRectangleAssistant.h.
  • Verification: Demonstration — build a rectangle assigning a different corner treatment to each corner and verify the generated contour.

FR-CAD-003 — Create parametric hole/pattern macros

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to create parametric geometry macros including single hole, oblong/slot (Langloch), hole row (Lochreihe), hole circle by centre/points/angle (Lochkreis), hole grid (Lochgitter), and round/square/rectangular/elliptical/tool-shaped/centre-punch patterns, each defined by editable numeric parameters and a local origin.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Macro/pattern creation is a primary productivity feature for perforated sheet parts.
  • Source: tc_bof/cad_assi.gritx macro masks (F_CadMacroDatenEinzelloch, ...Langloch, ...Lochgitter, ...LochkreisMitte, ...LochkreisPunkte, ...LochkreisWinkel, ...LochreiheStart/Ende/Abst, ...Quadrat, ...Rechteck, ...Rund, F_CadMacroZeileEllipse); tc_cad/cad_asm/macro.{hpp,cpp} (TeMacroTyp enum: e_round/e_rect/e_oblong/e_tool/e_vlg/e_ellipse/e_koerner/e_form); tc_dbase/dbase_command/{TcDbaseSingleHoleCommand,TcDbaseHoleRowCommand,TcDbaseHoleCircleCommand}.h; TcDbaseMacroAssistant.h.
  • Verification: Test — instantiate each macro type, edit its parameters, and confirm the emitted geometry.

FR-CAD-004 — Create and edit text (TrueType)

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to place text in the drawing using TrueType fonts and shall allow the text content, size and direction to be edited afterwards.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Text/marking annotation is required for labelling and engraving but is a distinct feature module.
  • Source: tc_bof/aCadCreateFunc.gritx aCadCreateText; tc_bof/aCadFunc.gritx aCadChangeText; tc_bof/tec_cad.gritx M_MenueZeichnungText; tc_cad/cad_ttf/{TcTTFString,TcLetter,TcContour,TcSegments}.h; tc_cad/cad_asm/cad_text.{hpp,cpp}; tc_dbase/dbase_command/{TcDbaseCreateTextCommand,TcDbaseChangeTextCommand}.h.
  • Verification: Test — create a text element, change its content/size/direction, verify rendered glyph geometry.

FR-CAD-005 — Geometric transformations (move, rotate, mirror, scale)

  • Requirement: The system shall transform selected elements by translation (two-point or horizontal/vertical), rotation (about a centre or two points), mirroring (about a horizontal/vertical/centre axis or two points) and scaling (about a centre or two points), and shall optionally leave the original in place to produce a transformed copy.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Move/rotate/mirror/scale are fundamental editing operations relied on in every drawing session.
  • Source: tc_bof/aCadFunc.gritx tCadMoveElem2Points, tCadMoveElemHorizontal, tCadMoveElemVertical, tCadMoveElemRotateCenter, tCadMoveElemRotate2Points, tCadMoveElemMirrorHorizontal/Vertical/Center/2Points, tCadMoveElemScaleCenter, tCadMoveElemScale2Points; tc_bof/cad_var.gritx F_CadVarKopieBewegen, F_CadVarKopieRotieren, F_CadVarKopieSpiegeln; tc_dbase/dbase_command/TcDbaseTransformCommand.h; tc_math/TcTransformationMatrix.h.
  • Verification: Test — apply each transformation with known parameters and verify element coordinates against the expected transform.

FR-CAD-006 — Stretch elements

  • Requirement: When the user selects a stretch reference and moves a defining point, the system shall stretch the affected elements while keeping connected geometry consistent, supporting free, horizontal-only and vertical-only stretch modes.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Stretching (Dehnen) is a key parametric-style edit for resizing features without rebuilding.
  • Source: tc_bof/aCadFunc.gritx aCadMoveElemStretch2Points, aCadMoveElemStretchHorizontal, aCadMoveElemStretchVertical; tc_bof/cad_var.gritx F_CadVarDehnen; tc_cad/cad_asm/dehnen.{hpp,cpp} (modes NORMAL/VER/HOR); tc_dbase/dbase_command/TcDbaseStretchCommand.h.
  • Verification: Test — stretch a rectangle edge by a known vector in each mode and verify resulting geometry.

FR-CAD-007 — Extend and trim elements

  • Requirement: The system shall extend an element to a picked target element or by a specified length, and shall trim elements to their intersection, using two-element extend and single-element extend operations.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Trimming/extending to close or clean corners is a core editing operation.
  • Source: tc_bof/aCadFunc.gritx aCadExtend2Elem, aCadExtendElem; tc_dbase/dbase_command/TcDbaseTrimCommand.h ("Kommando um Elemente zu trimmen"), TcDbaseAdjustCommand.h.
  • Verification: Test — extend/trim two lines to their intersection and confirm the corner is closed within tolerance.

FR-CAD-008 — Split and merge elements

  • Requirement: The system shall split an element at a picked position or at the intersection with another element, and shall merge two collinear/co-arc adjacent elements into a single element.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Split/merge is required to re-partition contours for downstream processing.
  • Source: tc_bof/aCadFunc.gritx aCadSplitElem, aCadSplit2Elem, aCadMergeElem; tc_cad/cad_asm/splitten.{hpp,cpp}; tc_dbase/dbase_command/{TcDbaseSplitCommand,TcDbaseMergeCommand,TcDbaseJoinCommand}.h.
  • Verification: Test — split a line at a point and re-merge the two halves; verify a single element results.

FR-CAD-009 — Delete elements, contours and redundant features

  • Requirement: The system shall delete individually picked elements, whole contours and current selections, and shall provide bulk cleanup deletions for small elements below a length threshold, small contours below a size threshold, and duplicate ("double") holes.
  • Priority: Essential

6. Assumptions & Constraints

  • Metric/inch duality — geometry tolerances are held in either metric or inch depending on gritunit (tc_math/TcToleranzContainer.h); parallel topsCad.org / topsCadInch.org and *Inch.Org config sets exist. Numeric behaviour must be consistent across unit systems. See [[50 - Constraints & Assumptions]].
  • Colour/line-style as layer surrogate — TruTops CAD has no named-layer object; classification is by a 16-entry colour palette plus line style. DXF layers are mapped onto colour/style at import (inferred from absence of any TcLayer/Ebene class and presence of F_DxfLayerFinder).
  • Legacy mixed stack — the interactive layer is a bespoke GRIT/MMK interpreter driving a C command lexer (cad_asm), a C++ command/memento database layer (tc_dbase), and a newer C#/.NET geometry kernel; all three cooperate on the same geometry.
  • Edition gating — some functions vary by edition (e.g. aCadMeltContours "Sonderoption für ToPs 400"; NoToPs600 mask variants; profile masks M_ProfilAbwicklungNoToPs600). Precise licensing rules were not confirmed from source.
  • Tolerance-driven algorithms — approximation, thinning, contour-closing and comparison all depend on user- or config-supplied tolerances; results are only correct relative to those tolerances.

7. Open Questions & Ambiguities

  • tc_forml scope — tc_forml/tc_forml.h (Formel_Sammlung) is a machine-technology formula collection (e.g. "X-Achse: Beschleunigung = f(Tafelgewicht)", enum "Von ToPs 300 verwendet"), not CAD parametric-variable formulas. The CAD parametric capability lives in cad_var.gritx + TcDbaseVarManager + Python. tc_forml likely belongs to the technology/bending area — see [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]] / [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]]. (Noted for the owning analyst.)
  • tc_geoPlan — appears to be a topological plan/graph model (Ecke/Element iterators); its precise role vs. tc_dbase geometry was not fully determined.
  • aTucadFunc.gritx / tc_rohr tube CAD — tube/profile CAD masks (aTucadCreate*, aTucadTechnoMirror*) belong to [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]]; tc_profil is shared (FR-CAD-noted profile creation/unfold), but the 3D tube editor is out of this area's scope.
  • GeoAlgorithmsKernel/Technologie — large portions (RemovalOrder, Processings, ProcessingOrder, TwinLine, Simulation, Sheet) are NC/nesting technology, not CAD geometry editing; documented under [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]] / [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]]. Only the pure GeoKernel geometry primitives (segments, curves, areas, intersection, hull, matrices, shape recognition) support this area.
  • Bending geometry in CAD — masks such as aCadMarkBendline, aCadCreateBending, aCadCreateBumping, aCadInvertBending, aCadChangeUnfold, aCadUnfoldProfile create/manage bend lines and unfolding inside the CAD editor. The geometric marking is in scope, but the bending technology/parameters belong to [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]]; the boundary was not sharply resolved.
  • Shape recognition — GeoKernel/GeoElements/{TcGeoShapeRecognizer,TcGeoShapesBuilder}.cs recognises higher-level shapes; the extent to which this feeds the interactive CAD editor (vs. NC technology) was not confirmed.
  • Grid/raster of circles — aCadChangeCircleGrid, M_CadKreiseRastern suggest a circle-gridding tool; its exact semantics (rasterise circle into a grid of holes?) were inferred, not verified.
