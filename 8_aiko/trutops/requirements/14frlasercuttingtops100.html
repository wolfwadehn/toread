FR — Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)

Reverse-engineered requirement draft — derived from static analysis of legacy source, NOT an authoritative specification. Treat SHALL statements as reconstructed intent.

1. Purpose & Scope

This document specifies the functional requirements for laser-cutting technology programming in the TruTops CAD/CAM suite — the capability historically branded ToPs 100 (2D flat-sheet laser and combined laser/punch machines) and its viewer/CAD companions ToPs 400 Cad and ToPs 2000. The area covers the full path from an unmachined nested sheet to a collision-checked, sequenced, machine-ready cutting job: assigning cutting technology to contours, generating lead-in/lead-out (approach/retract) geometry, piercing, micro-joints / bridges / micro-welds, marking and engraving, cutting-order and positioning-path optimization, sheet dividing and remainder-sheet handling, cut simulation, and the automation of part removal (unloading, sorting, ejection) via lift/holding devices.

The primary evidence modules are tc_las (the laser technology engine, ~590k LOC, split into las_asm command automata, las_tech technology/interaction/object layers, las_simu graphical simulation, las_util), tc_lift (loading & unloading automation: suction cups, grippers, lift frames) and tc_liftMachineSimulationData. The interactive command/dialog set lives in the GRIT masks aLasAllg, aLasProcess, aLasOptimize, aLasInteract, aLasFunc, aLasGTS, aLasLift and the shared AutoCut engine tec_acut. Technology is stored in the Laser SQL database (LASERTECHNO*, PROZESSOPTIONEN, REGEL*, MAS_STEGE, STEG_TYP, SORT_*).

Cross-cutting concerns (nesting/palletizing → [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]], NC output & motion optimization → [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]], the shared technology database → [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], punching side of combined machines → [[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]]) are referenced but owned by those documents.

2. Capability Overview

  • Load & prepare a part, nested sheet or production order for laser processing; select the machine and cutting technology table (LTT).
  • Assign cutting technology to contours automatically (per-part Automat / AutoCut) or interactively, driven by a selectable ruleset (Regelwerk) and machine-/material-/thickness-dependent technology tables (LASERTECHNO).
  • Lead-in / lead-out (Anfahren / Wegfahren): configurable geometry (line/arc), position, pre-cut, and interactive editing (move, turn, invert), plus cut-end optimization.
  • Piercing (Einstechen) type and parameter assignment (single-/multi-stage, ramp, blow-out).
  • Part-retention features: ToPs micro-joints, machine micro-joints, micro-welds, and configurable bridges / webs (Stege) with named layouts.
  • Contour-size-dependent technology (small / medium / large classes) and kerf compensation (Bahnkorrektur, Kreiskorrektur) and corner treatment (Eckenbehandlung).
  • Common cut via part groups (Teilegruppe) and shared cutting lines.
  • Marking / engraving: arbitrary text, labeling cycles, and DataMatrix codes.
  • Sheet dividing (Tafelschnitt), scrap dicing (Abfallzerschnitt) and remainder-sheet (Resttafel) creation.
  • Gas & cooling parameters (gas type, pressure, nozzle distance, corner-cooling time).
  • Cutting-sequence & positioning-path optimization including fly-cut, and collision detection.
  • Process options / part-support strategy (falling, tilting, hooking, stable) and two-head / TwinLine processing.
  • Sheet repositioning (Nachsetzen) for oversized sheets; sheet measurement (Blechvermessung).
  • Graphical cut simulation (GTS).
  • Part-removal automation: unloading with holding devices (suction/gripper/magnet), sorting to compartments/pallets (SortMaster), and part ejection via catcher/flap.

4. Functional Requirements

FR-LASER-001 — Load part, sheet or order for laser processing

  • Requirement: When the user selects a part, nested sheet or production order to open, the system shall load its geometry into the laser workspace and make it available for cutting-technology assignment.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Nothing can be programmed until the workpiece is loaded; this is the entry point of the laser workflow.
  • Source: tc_bof/aLasAllg.gritx masks aLasDateiLoad, aLasDateiLoadSheet, aLasDateiLoadAuftrag, aLasDateiLoadAndProcessSheet, aLasDateiLoadAndMirror, aLasSelectFile; tc_las/las_asm/t1_load.*, las_eins/init_las.cpp.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-002 — Assign a cutting technology table (LTT)

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to select and apply a cutting technology table matched to the active machine, material and sheet thickness, and shall display the currently active table to the user.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: All cut parameters (power, speed, gas, focus, nozzle) are resolved from the technology table; correct cutting is impossible without it.
  • Source: aLasProcess.gritx masks aLasSetWerkzeug, aLasShowRegelwerk; aLasAllg.gritx aLasSelectWerkzeug/aLasSelectLastWerkzeug; asm_call(ltt_change …); SQL Laser.LASERTECHNO, Laser.LASERTECHNO2, Laser.LASERTAB; tc_las/las_tech/db/*. See [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]].
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-003 — Select and apply a cutting ruleset (Regelwerk)

  • Requirement: Where automatic technology assignment is used, the system shall allow the user to choose a ruleset that governs how cut/mark technology, lead-ins, piercing and part-support are derived, and shall use the selected ruleset for subsequent automatic processing.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The ruleset encodes site-/machine-specific automation policy separate from the raw parameter tables; it is machine-type dependent (a distinct H2O variant exists).
  • Source: aLasProcess.gritx mask aLasSetRegelwerk (M_RegelwerkAuswaehlen, M_H2O_RegelwerkAuswaehlen); SQL Laser.REGEL_ALLG, Laser.REGEL10…REGEL201.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-004 — Automatic per-part cutting-technology generation

  • Requirement: When the user requests processing, the system shall automatically generate the complete cutting technology (contour roles, lead-ins, piercing, sequence) for all parts, for only the unprocessed parts, or for a single picked part/contour, as selected.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Bulk automatic technology assignment (the laser Automat) is the primary production workflow; per-part/per-contour scope selection is a core convenience.
  • Source: aLasProcess.gritx masks tLasProcessAll, tLasProcessSingle, aShowMenuBearbErzeugen; asm_call(automat draw … automat <all|new|job>); tc_las/las_tech/auto/* (la_new.cpp, la_kont.cpp).
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-005 — AutoCut automatic processing configuration

  • Requirement: Where AutoCut is licensed, the system shall let the user configure and run automatic processing that assigns, per configurable contour selection, the operations cut, mark, center-mark (Körnen), coating-removal (Abdampfen) and part ejection, using named, savable AutoCut configurations.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: AutoCut is the shared laser/punch/combined automatic-processing engine driven by reusable named configurations; it is an add-on capability.
  • Source: tc_bof/tec_acut.gritx masks FUNC_ACUT_Start, FUNC_ACUT_EnableLaser, M_AutoCutKonfigurationLaser, M_AutoLaserParam, F_AutoCutKonfig_Laser; SQL Punch.AUTOCUT_KONFIG; tc_tec/tec_asm/*.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-006 — Contour role assignment: cut vs. mark

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the user to designate, per contour or per part, whether the contour is to be cut or to be marked/engraved rather than cut.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Distinguishing cutting from marking contours is fundamental to producing a correct program (e.g. engraved labels must not be cut through).
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_kennzeichnen.* (TcKennzeichnenDatenHalter), las_tech/db/TcKonturenSucher; AutoCut O_AcutSchneiden / O_AcutKennzeichnen in tec_acut.gritx.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-007 — Lead-in (Anfahren) geometry and parameters

  • Requirement: The system shall generate a lead-in path for each cut contour whose geometric form (straight line and/or arc), length, angle and pre-cut are derived from technology and are editable per contour.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: The pierce point must sit off the finished contour; lead-in geometry and its parameters directly determine cut-edge quality.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_anwegfahren.* (TcAnWegfahrenDatenHalter, enum e_anfahren); las_tech/sonst/la_sanfa.* (la_anfahren_an_line_arc_cir, la_bilde_anfa_vorschnitt); aLasInteract.gritx aLasChangeAnfahrform, aLasChangeAnfahrparameterLeft/Right.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-008 — Lead-out (Wegfahren) geometry and parameters

  • Requirement: The system shall allow generation and editing of a lead-out (retract) path per cut contour, including its geometric form and parameters, independently of the lead-in.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Lead-outs are needed for specific edge/overtravel requirements but are not present on every contour, so they are separately controllable.
  • Source: aLasInteract.gritx masks aLasChangeWegfahrform, aLasChangeWegfParameterLeft/Right; asm_call(laser modify way … point wegfa); enum e_wegfahren in las_anwegfahren.h.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-009 — Interactive lead-in/lead-out manipulation

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user interactively move, rotate, invert (swap direction) and reposition an existing lead-in/lead-out on a picked contour without regenerating the whole part.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Manual fine-tuning of approach geometry is a convenience for edge cases the automatic rules do not cover.
  • Source: aLasInteract.gritx masks aLasInvertAnfahren, aLasMoveAnfahren, aLasMove2Pts, aLasTurn2Pts, aLasMoveHorizontal, aLasMoveVertical, aLasTurnMitte, aLasTurnSchneidlinieCL/CCL.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-010 — Piercing (Einstechen) type and parameter assignment

  • Requirement: The system shall assign a piercing type and its parameters (focus, ramp, nozzle distance, blow-out time, gas) to each cut start, allow the user to change the piercing type, and allow a piercing to be deleted.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Correct piercing prevents dross and burn-through at cut initiation; it is a mandatory element of every laser cut.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_eins.* (TcEinstechAutomatAttribute.attEinstechArt); aLasInteract.gritx aLasSetEinstechart, aLasDeleteEinstechen (M_KonturEinstechen, M_H2O_Einstechen); asm_call(laser pierce …); SQL LASERTECHNO columns LT_EINST_*.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-011 — Contour-size-dependent cutting technology

  • Requirement: The system shall select cutting parameters (power, speed, gas pressure, focus, nozzle distance, pulse frequency) according to the size class of each contour (small / medium / large), and shall let the user override the size class per contour.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Small inner contours require reduced parameters to avoid heat build-up; the technology table carries distinct small/medium/large parameter sets.
  • Source: SQL LASERTECHNO column groups LT_SCH_KL_* (klein), LT_SCH_MI_* (mittel), LT_SCH_GR_* (groß) with NOR/RED variants; aLasInteract.gritx aLasSetKonturgroesse, tLasSetSwitchKonturgroesse; aLasFunc.gritx tLasShowGleicheKonturgroesse; asm_call(las_set size …).
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-012 — ToPs micro-joints

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user place and delete program-controlled (ToPs) micro-joints on contours, at the contour end or at an arbitrary picked position, with a configurable micro-joint length.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Micro-joints keep parts from tipping/dropping during cutting; they are a common but optional retention feature.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_microjoint.*; aLasInteract.gritx aLasCreateMicrojoint, tLasDeleteMicrojoint (variables tmp_kpMicrojointLaenge, tmp_kpMjEndeOderBel_Radio).
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-013 — Machine micro-joints

  • Requirement: Where the machine control supports machine-side micro-joints, the system shall let the user place, lock and unlock machine micro-joints, reading the micro-joint length from the ruleset.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Machine micro-joints are executed by the control rather than the program and are only available on capable controls; locking prevents automatic removal.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_maschmicrojoint.*; aLasInteract.gritx aLasSperreMaschmicrojoint, aLasEntsperreMaschmicrojoint; asm_call(microjoint read 'tmp_kpMaschMicrojointLaenge'); SQL PROZESSOPTIONEN.PO_MIJO_TYP.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-014 — Micro-welds

  • Requirement: Where micro-weld technology is available, the system shall let the user create and delete micro-welds that re-weld a part to the surrounding material after cutting.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Micro-welds are an alternative retention method for specific materials/machines; not universally available.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_mwld.*; aLasInteract.gritx aLasCreateMicroweld, tLasDeleteMicroweld.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-015 — Bridge / web placement and layouts

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user place bridges (uncut webs) in configurable patterns, choose, save and re-apply named bridge layouts, and select the bridge type and material-dependent bridge width.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Bridges stabilise scrap grids and thin structures; reusable named layouts (Stegbelegung) are a productivity feature for repeated part families.
  • Source: aLasOptimize.gritx masks aLasSetStege, aLasChooseStegbelegung, aLasSaveStegbelegung; SQL Laser.MAS_STEGE, Laser.STEG_TYP, Laser.MAS_STEGTYPEN, Laser.STEG_WERKSTOFF.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-016 — Kerf / path correction (Bahnkorrektur)

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user set and delete path (kerf) correction on selected contours so that the tool path is offset by the beam kerf to achieve nominal part dimensions.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Kerf compensation is needed where the control does not apply it, or where per-contour override is required.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_bahnkorrektur.*; aLasInteract.gritx tLasSetBahnkorrektur, tLasDeleteBahnkorrektur.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-017 — Circle / hole kerf correction (Kreiskorrektur)

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user create and delete a dedicated circle correction for holes so bore diameters are cut to nominal size.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Small holes need special radial correction distinct from general path correction to meet diameter tolerance.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_cico.* (init_las_circle_correction_befehl); aLasInteract.gritx aLasCreateKreiskorrektur, tLasDeleteKreiskorrektur.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-018 — Corner treatment (Eckenbehandlung)

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user create, change and delete corner treatments (e.g. loops, dwell/cooling, rounding) at contour corners.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Sharp corners overheat; corner treatments (loop-out, cooling dwell) preserve corner quality and are machine/material dependent.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/TcAsmEckenBehandlung.*; aLasInteract.gritx tLasCreateEckenbehandlung, tLasDeleteEckenbehandlung; SQL LASERTECHNO.LT_SCH_ECKENK_ZEIT (corner-cooling time).
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-019 — Common cut via part groups

  • Requirement: Where common-cut is applicable, the system shall let the user form part groups so that adjacent parts share a single cutting line, and shall display which parts belong to the same group.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Common cutting reduces cut length and pierces on tightly nested identical parts; it requires geometrically compatible neighbours.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_teilegruppen.* ("Automat, um Teilegruppen zu erzeugen und anzeigen"); aLasInteract.gritx tLasCreateTeilegruppe, aLasShowTeilegruppe; common-cut references in las_tech/interakt/la_iallg.*, la_ianf2.cpp.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-020 — Marking, labeling and DataMatrix engraving

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user add laser marking to a sheet/part in the form of arbitrary text, labeling cycles and DataMatrix codes, and shall delete such markings on request.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Part identification (text / 2D codes) is a common traceability add-on distinct from cutting; it depends on marking-capable technology.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_btxt.*, TcLasDataMatrixAsm.*; aLasInteract.gritx aLasCreateBelText, aLasCreateDataMatrix, tLasDeleteDataMatrix, tLasCreateBelZyklus; asm_call(bel_text create …); masks M_DataMatrix, M_DataMatrixTube.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-021 — Sheet dividing cut and scrap dicing

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user create, change and delete sheet dividing cuts (Tafelschnitt) and automatic scrap dicing (Abfallzerschnitt) that separate the raw sheet and cut the residual skeleton into removable pieces.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Dividing cuts and scrap dicing make the skeleton removable and enable off-cut reuse; needed on larger sheets/automation.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_cut.* (S_Las_cut, TeCutType); las_tech/interakt/TcTrennschnittManager.*, las_tech/sonst/TcTafelTrennschnittSortierer.*; aLasInteract.gritx aLasCreateTafelschnitt, aLasDeleteTafelschnitt, tLasCutAbfall, aLasCutAbfallAuto; asm_call(las_cut …).
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-022 — Remainder-sheet creation

  • Requirement: When a dividing cut separates reusable material, the system shall create a remainder sheet according to a configurable direction, minimum size and growth increment.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Reclaiming usable off-cuts as remainder sheets reduces material cost.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/TcRemainderSheetCreator.* (TeSheetCreationGuideline, m_minSizeOfRemainingSheet, m_sheetIncreaseIncrement) referenced from las_cut.hpp.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-023 — Gas and cooling technology parameters

  • Requirement: The system shall apply gas type, gas pressure, nozzle distance and corner-cooling time from the technology table to each cut and pierce, differentiated by normal/reduced regime.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Assist-gas selection and pressure are decisive for cut quality and are integral to every laser cut.
  • Source: SQL LASERTECHNO columns LT_SCH_*_GASART, LT_SCH_*_GASDRUCK, LT_SCH_*_DUESENABST, LT_ALL_GASSP_SCH_EIN/GASSP_EIN_SCH, LT_EINST_NOR_GASART, LT_SCH_ECKENK_ZEIT; tc_las/las_tech/db/*.
  • Verification: Inspection

FR-LASER-024 — Cutting-sequence and positioning-path optimization

  • Requirement: The system shall compute a cutting sequence and machine positioning path for the sheet, shall let the user optimize it automatically or reorder operations manually, and shall support fly-cut positioning where enabled.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Sequence and rapid-travel order directly determine cycle time and thermal safety; fly-cut cuts non-productive time on grid holes.
  • Source: tc_las/las_tech/sequ/* (se_rechne.*, se_berei.*); tc_las/las_asm/las_optimieren.*, las_optidata.*; aLasOptimize.gritx aLasOptimiereAuto, aLasSetPosiwayAuto/Manu, tLasSetPosireihenfolge, tLasUmordne*, tLasCreateFlycut, aLasShowFlycut; SQL PROZESSOPTIONEN.PO_FLYCUT*, Laser.OPTIMIERER. See [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]].
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-025 — Collision detection and reporting

  • Requirement: The system shall check the generated cutting program for collisions between the cutting head and already-processed parts/tilted parts, and shall report detected collisions to the user via a collision log.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Head collisions with tipped-up parts damage the machine; detection with configurable clearances is safety-critical.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_kol.*; aLasProcess.gritx aLasShowKollision, aLasShowKollisionsprotokoll; aLasAllg.gritx tLasParamPartKollision, tLasParamSheetKollision; aLasFunc.gritx tLasShowKollisions.
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-026 — Process options and part-support strategy

  • Requirement: The system shall let the user select a named process option that defines the part-support/separation strategy (e.g. falling, tilting, hooking, stable), the automatic-part-separation (APS) mode and micro-joint type, and shall apply it during processing.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: How a cut part is supported and released is machine-/geometry-dependent and governs downstream removal; it is bundled into reusable process options.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/las_prozessoptionData.*; aLasProcess.gritx aLasSetProzessoptionen (M_Prozessoptionen); SQL Laser.PROZESSOPTIONEN (PO_*_SUPPORT_FALLEND/KIPPEND/HAKEND/STABIL, PO_K_APS_MODE, PO_MIJO_TYP, PO_ANF_WINKELKOR).
  • Verification: Test

FR-LASER-027 — Two-head / TwinLine processing

  • Requirement: Where the machine has multiple cutting heads (two-head or TwinLine), the system shall let the user create, view, split and delete multi-head processing with a configurable head offset and active-head selection.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Multi-head machines double throughput but require dedicated offset/assignment programming; only available on such machines.
  • Source: aLasInteract.gritx aLasCreate2Kopfbearb, aLasSplit2Kopf, aLasShow2Kopfbearb, aLasDelete2Kopfbearb; asm_call(multi_head set …); tc_las/las_tech/twinLine/* (TcTwinLineVerwalter, TcTwinLineNestGeometrie); aLasGTS.gritx aLasTwinLineEinfachStart.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-028 — Sheet repositioning (Nachsetzen)

  • Requirement: Where the part exceeds the machine travel, the system shall let the user define repositioning (Nachsetzen) operations and shall display the reachable tolerance zones for the repositioned areas.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Oversized sheets must be re-gripped/re-clamped and re-referenced; only relevant when geometry exceeds the working envelope.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/TcAsmLasReposition.* ("Automat für Nachsetzenfunktionalität … Anzeigen der Toleranzbereiche"); aLasInteract.gritx aLasCreateReposit; SQL Laser.VERFAHRBARKEIT.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-029 — Automatic part unloading with holding devices

  • Requirement: Where a loading/unloading automation is configured, the system shall create unloading operations that pick good parts and scrap with holding devices (suction cups, magnetic and hole grippers) mounted on lift frames, and shall let the user add, change, copy, rotate and delete these operations.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Automated part removal is the core of lights-out operation on automation-equipped machines; device geometry must be validated against the part.
  • Source: tc_lift/* (TcBasisLift, TcSauger, TcAbschaelsauger, TcTragsauger, TcMagnetgreifer, TcHoleGripper, TcLiftRahmen, TcSheetUnloadLiftFacade); tc_las/las_tech/maschine/TcEntladeManager.*, TcEntladeVorgang.*, TcEntladeGutteil.*, TcEntladeAbfall.*; aLasLift.gritx aLasCreateEntladenAuto, tLasChangeEntladen, tLasCopyEntladen, tLasDeleteEntladen, aLasAdjustSauger, tLasTurnLiftCL/CCL; SQL Laser.BASISLIFT, Laser.EINZELSAUGER, Laser.RAHMEN, Laser.MAP_MAS_LIFT.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-030 — Part sorting to compartments / pallets

  • Requirement: Where a sorting system (e.g. SortMaster) is configured, the system shall assign unloaded parts to sorting compartments/pallets according to a selectable unloading/sorting strategy, and shall support compartment weight limits.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Sorting parts to defined destinations is required for automated post-processing; strategy and weight checks are configuration dependent.
  • Source: tc_las/las_tech/maschine/TcSortmaster.*, TcEntladeStrategie* (NachAdapter, NachBearbeitung); aLasLift.gritx aLasSetSortMasterAdapter; SQL Laser.SORT_FACH (SORT_FACH_MAX_ZUL_GEWICHT, SORT_FACH_GEWICHT_PRUEFEN), Laser.SORT_PALETTE, Laser.SORT_EINHEIT, Laser.PALETTENTYP.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-031 — Part ejection via catcher / flap

  • Requirement: Where the machine has a part flap/chute or catcher, the system shall let the user program part ejection (Ausschleusen) of small parts through the flap/catcher and shall validate the part against the ejection opening.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Small parts are removed by dropping/ejecting rather than gripping; the target opening constrains which parts qualify.
  • Source: tc_las/las_tech/interakt/TcLasCatcher.* ("das Fangen von Teilen, Konturen"); aLasInteract.gritx aLasNachbCatcher; tec_acut.gritx O_AcutAusschleusenLaser, M_AutoRutschenPruef, APS fields O_AcutApsKlappe/O_AcutApsMatrize/O_AcutApsLift.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-032 — Graphical cut simulation (GTS)

  • Requirement: The system shall provide a graphical simulation of the generated cutting job that the user can start, step forward/backward, pause, continue and stop, visualising cut order, lead-ins, piercing and part movement.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Simulation lets programmers validate sequence, collisions and part release before running the machine.
  • Source: tc_las/las_simu/* (gd_simu, gd_anfah, gd_einst, gd_ecke, gd_entso); aLasGTS.gritx aLasStartSimuGts, aLasStepSimuGts, aLasStepSimuBackGts, aLasHaltSimuGts, aLasContinueSimuGts, aLasEndSimuGts; tc_las/las_asm/las_gts.*, gd_simu.*.
  • Verification: Demonstration

FR-LASER-033 — Sheet measurement (Blechvermessung)

  • Requirement: Where sheet-measurement is supported, the system shall let the user create, parameterise and delete a sheet-measurement operation that probes the actual sheet position/edges before cutting.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Measuring the real sheet position compensates loading inaccuracy on remnant/irregular sheets; it is machine-capability dependent.
  • Source: tc_las/las_asm/TcAsmSheetMeasure.*; aLasInteract.gritx tLasCreateSheetMeasure, tLasChangeSheetMeasureParams, tLasDeleteSheetMeasure.
  • Verification: Test

5. External Interfaces (area-specific)

Full specifications in [[30 - External Interface Requirements]].

  • Laser technology database (SQL Server / MDB): Laser.* tables — LASERTECHNO, LASERTECHNO2, LASERTECHNOBC, LASERTAB, LASERTOPS, PROZESSOPTIONEN, REGEL_ALLG/REGEL10…201, MAS_STEGE, STEG_TYP, MAS_STEGTYPEN, STEG_WERKSTOFF, VOREINSTELLUNG, OPTIMIERER, SORT_FACH/SORT_PALETTE/SORT_EINHEIT, BASISLIFT/EINZELSAUGER/RAHMEN/MAP_MAS_LIFT, MASCHINE/MAS_ALLG/MAS_GRUPPE. Read/written via tc_las/las_tech/db/* and tc_dbase. See [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]].
  • Machine controls: technology and NC output is control-version dependent (STEUERUNGSVERSIONEN, NC_KOPFTAB_MASCHINE, NCZYKLEN); a distinct water-jet control type (H2O) exists (WASSER01…05, M_H2O_* masks). NC generation itself is owned by [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]].
  • Nested-sheet input: parts/sheets/orders arrive from nesting/part management (aLasDateiLoad*). See [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]] and [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]].
  • External laser DLLs: tc_las/LaserDlls (LaserCInterface, LaserDotNetInterface) — bridge to external/native laser technology libraries.
  • Lift simulation data: tc_liftMachineSimulationData — geometry/kinematics for unloading-device simulation.

6. Assumptions & Constraints

Cross-cutting items are consolidated in [[50 - Constraints & Assumptions]].

  • Edition gating: Laser cutting is the ToPs 100 edition; ToPs400Cad is the CAD/viewer companion and ToPs2000 a related edition (ini_files/ToPs400, ToPs400Cad, ToPs2000; tops_applic_nummer_var=400). Many requirements above are Conditional because they depend on licensed options (AutoCut, automation/lift, TwinLine, sorting) or on machine capability.
  • Legacy architecture: the laser engine is a stack of command automata (las_asm/*) driven by the GRIT/MMK interpreter (aLas* masks calling asm_call(...)), backed by an SQL technology database. Requirements are reconstructed from mask logic, header intent and table schemas, not from a specification.
  • Units: parallel _inch table variants exist (Laser_inch.*); the system supports metric and imperial technology sets.
  • Shared engine: AutoCut (tec_acut) and parts of the automation/NC path are shared with punching/combined machines ([[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]]); this document scopes only the laser behaviour.

7. Open Questions & Ambiguities

  • GTS acronym: aLasGTS/las_gts provide preparation and step simulation and reference the kerf gap (la_check_schnittspalt_for_gts); the exact expansion of "GTS" (graphical technology simulation vs. a specific processing pass) could not be confirmed from source. It may partly overlap the general viewer/simulation area ([[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]]).
  • Water-jet (H2O): a full parallel technology (WASSER01…05, M_H2O_*, MaschineTechnoTyp == c_H2o) lives inside the laser module. It is treated here as a conditional variant of laser cutting; whether it warrants its own functional area is a scoping question for the index owner.
  • AutoCut ownership: tec_acut masks cover laser, punch and combined modes in one engine; the split of requirements between [[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]] and this document at the shared-configuration level may need reconciliation.
  • Tube laser: aLasCreateTubecut/M_DataMatrixTube and APPLIC_NUMMER=='400' branches indicate tube-laser paths reachable from the same masks; tube machining is owned by [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]] — the boundary for shared laser primitives is unclear.
  • APS vs. lift vs. catcher: automatic part separation (APS), lift/suction unloading and flap/catcher ejection appear as three overlapping removal mechanisms (PO_K_APS_MODE, tc_lift, TcLasCatcher); their precise selection precedence and mutual exclusivity were not fully determined.
  • Bahnkorrektur precedence: whether kerf compensation (FR-LASER-016) is applied by TruTops or delegated to the control varies by control version; the decision rule was not extracted.
