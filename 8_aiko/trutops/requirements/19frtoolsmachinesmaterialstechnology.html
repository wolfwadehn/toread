product: trutopscad org: trumpf title: "FR — Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology" doc: "19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology" id-prefix: "FR-TECH" srs-standard: "ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018" status: reverse-engineered-draft source: "TruTopsCAD legacy source snapshot (files dated May 2024)" related: ["[[00 - Index & Reading Guide]]", "[[04 - Domain Model & Glossary]]", "[[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]]", "[[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]]", "[[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]]", "[[30 - External Interface Requirements]]"] tags: [requirements, trutops, functional-requirements]

FR — Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology

Reverse-engineered requirement draft — derived from static analysis of legacy source, NOT an authoritative specification. Treat SHALL statements as reconstructed intent.

1. Purpose & Scope

This document specifies the master-data and technology management capabilities of the TruTops CAD/CAM suite: the persistent catalogs and parameter tables that describe what a shop can physically do and that drive the punching, laser and tube processors. Concretely it covers:

  • Machine definitions — machine/control identity, work area and sheet capacity, axis dynamics, peripheral equipment (clamps, tool changer, loading/unloading automation, scrap handling), control-version compatibility, and machine rule sets (Regelwerk).
  • Tool catalogs — punch tools, dies (Matrizen), multi-tools, thread formers, laser matrices, cassettes/tool holders, together with per-tool technology parameters, tool lists, and the connection to the networked/central tool management (ITM / SW06).
  • Material & thickness definitions — materials (Werkstoffe) with physical/technological properties, raw materials (material + thickness), sheet/stock inventory items (Lagergut), material groups and pricing, and allowed machining types.
  • Technology tables — laser technology tables (LTT) for cutting/piercing/marking/micro-joining, punch/laser AutoCut configurations, micro-joint (Steg) definitions, and tube technology & profile catalogs.

The area is the shared foundation beneath the process-specific documents ([[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]], [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]], [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]], [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]], [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]]). All product editions rely on some of this master data; the ini-file tree (ini_files/ToPs100, ToPs100Lite, ToPs2000, Tops300, ToPs400, ToPs400Cad, ToPsCad, ToPsNest) shows which editions surface which technology, so most tool/technology capabilities are Conditional (edition- or licence-dependent), while machine and material master data is broadly Essential.

Primary evidence: tc_wzg (tools, ~72k LOC), tc_maschine, tc_materialVerwaltung, tc_mtl, tc_tec; the gritx dialogs under tc_bof (tec_wzg.gritx, tec_acut.gritx, tec_allg.gritx, datenteil/te1_mas.gritx, datenteil/te3_ltt.gritx, datenteil/te3_wzg.gritx); and the SQL master tables under Databases/SQLServerDatabase/Tables in the Punch, Laser, Tube and Common schemas.

2. Capability Overview

  • Identify each machine uniquely by name + machine-type + control + control-version (TcMaschinenSchluessel), with official-name / prototype-alias resolution.
  • Store machine work area, overrun/traverse area, maximum sheet size, and per-axis velocity/acceleration limits.
  • Configure machine peripheral equipment (clamps, tool changer, measuring system, loading/unloading lift & suction systems, grip, scrap gate, sort unit) via MAS_ALLG.
  • Create, edit, copy and browse machine definitions through the technology (T2) machine-data dialogs.
  • Validate machine/control version compatibility and enforce machine rule sets (Regelwerk) with min/max bounds.
  • Compute sheet loading positions from selectable loading strategies (TcBeladeStratege).
  • Maintain punch-tool records (WZG3) with full CRUD, classified by a rich tool-type taxonomy (round, rectangular, oblong, forming, roller, marking, multi-cut, thread cutter, flow drill …).
  • Manage multi-tools / multi-cut, dies (standard / slanted / integrated-flattening), thread formers with per-material/thickness assignment, cassettes, and per-tool technology parameters.
  • Maintain named tool lists, import/export tools as XML, and connect to central/networked tool management (ITM/SW06) with tool-availability checking.
  • Filter and sort the tool inventory by many criteria for automatic tool assignment.
  • Maintain materials (physical + technological properties, international designations), raw materials (thickness, foil, coating, tool clearance), sheet/stock items, material groups with pricing, allowed machining types, and material aliases / LTT mappings.
  • Maintain laser technology tables (LTT) and AutoCut configurations, micro-joint (Steg) definitions, and tube technology & profile catalogs.

4. Functional Requirements

FR-TECH-001 — Unique machine identification key

  • Requirement: The system shall identify every machine definition by the tuple (machine designation, machine type, control designation, control version) and shall treat that tuple as the unique key when reading, comparing and selecting machine data.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Every technology, tool, rule and NC-header record is keyed on this tuple; without a unique machine identity the whole master-data model is ambiguous.
  • Source: tc_maschine/TcMaschinenSchluessel.h (constructor + isSameMaschine, operator<); Punch.MASCHINE columns MA_NAME, MA_TYP, MA_STEUERUNG, MA_ST_VERSION; identical key columns in Laser.MASCHINE, Tube.MASCHINE.
  • Verification: Test — create two machines differing only in control version and confirm they are stored and retrieved as distinct records.

FR-TECH-002 — Machine work area, traverse area and sheet capacity

  • Requirement: The system shall store, per machine, the working-area extents (X/Y/Z), the overrun/traverse-area offsets (±X/±Y/±Z), and the maximum processable sheet dimensions, and shall make these available to nesting, motion and loading calculations.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: The work area and maximum sheet size bound every nesting and NC-motion decision.
  • Source: Punch.MASCHINE columns MA_ARBEITB_X/Y/Z, MA_UEBERFA_XP/XM/YP/YM/ZP/ZM, MA_MAXBLECH_X/Y/Z; tc_maschine/TcBeladeStratege.h (getArbeitsbereichBreite, getUeberfahrbereichBreite, getTafelBreite).
  • Verification: Analysis — inspect stored machine record and confirm work-area/sheet-size values are read by nesting and loading modules.

FR-TECH-003 — Per-axis velocity and acceleration limits

  • Requirement: The system shall store and return the minimum and maximum velocity and acceleration for the machine axes (X, Y, Z and, where present, A/B), so that motion-time estimation and optimization can respect the machine's dynamic limits.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Cutting/positioning time and motion optimization ([[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]]) depend on axis dynamics.
  • Source: tc_maschine/TcMaschinenSchluessel.h enum E_MinMaxValueOfMachine (e_minVelocityInX … e_maxAccelerationInB) and getMinMaxValueOfMachine; Punch.MASCHINE MA_GESMAX_*, MA_GESMIN_*, MA_BESMAX_*, MA_BESMIN_*, MA_BESOFF_*, MA_BESSTEI_*.
  • Verification: Test — query each axis value via getMinMaxValueOfMachine and confirm it matches the database record.

FR-TECH-004 — Machine peripheral-equipment configuration

  • Requirement: Where a machine is equipped with automation or peripheral devices, the system shall record their configuration — clamps (Pratzen), support (Auflage), tool changer, measuring system, scrap gate (Schrottweiche), loading and disposal lift/suction systems, grip, flap (Klappe), chute (Rutsche) and sort unit — as part of the machine's general data.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Loading, unloading, disposal and collision behaviour depend on which peripherals a specific machine carries; only automation-equipped machines populate these.
  • Source: Punch.MAS_ALLG columns (MA_ALLG_PRATZEN, MA_ALLG_WZG_WECHSLER, MA_ALLG_MESS_SYSTEM, MA_ALLG_SCHROTTWEICHE, MA_ALLG_BELADE_SYS, MA_ALLG_ENTSORGE_SYS, MA_ALLG_GRIP_TYP, MA_ALLG_KLAPPE, MA_ALLG_RUTSCHE, MA_ALLG_SORTIER_EIN, MA_ALLG_STANZKOPF, MA_ALLG_ABSAUGUNG …); gritx datenteil/te1_mas.gritx masks M_TECHNO_MA_SpanntechnikBase/Change/Create, M_TECHNO_MA_ANSCHLAEGE.
  • Verification: Demonstration — configure a machine with a lift/suction loading system and confirm the equipment is persisted and offered to the loading dialogs.

FR-TECH-005 — Machine definition management (create / edit / copy / browse)

  • Requirement: The system shall provide interactive dialogs to create, edit, copy and browse machine definitions and their subsidiary data (base data, limits, laser data, tube data, clamping technology, stops, support bars, two-head configuration).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Machine master data must be maintainable by the technology administrator without direct database editing.
  • Source: gritx datenteil/te1_mas.gritx masks M_TECHNO_MA_ANLEGEN, M_TECHNO_MA_KOPIEREN, M_TECHNO_MA_AUSWAHL, M_TECHNO_MA_MASCHINENDATEN, M_TECHNO_MA_GRUND1/2/3, M_TECHNO_MA_LASER1/2/3, M_TECHNO_MA_ROHR, M_TECHNO_MA_ZWEIKOPF, M_TECHNO_MA_GRENZ_100/150.
  • Verification: Demonstration — create a machine via the dialog and confirm it is persisted and selectable.

FR-TECH-006 — Control-version compatibility validation

  • Requirement: The system shall record, per machine/control/function, the permitted minimum and maximum versions of the NC, MMC, PLC, DIAS and laser-control software, and shall use these ranges to validate machine/control combinations.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: NC output and option availability differ by controller software version; incompatible combinations must be detected.
  • Source: Punch.STEUERUNGSVERSIONEN columns (STV_NC_MIN/MAX_VERSION, STV_MMC_*, STV_PLC_*, STV_DIAS_*, STV_LASERSTEU_*, STV_STEUERUNG_*, STV_OPTION); Punch.STEUERUNGSVERSIONEN_CACHED.
  • Verification: Test — select a control version outside the stored range and confirm the combination is rejected or flagged.

FR-TECH-007 — Machine rule sets (Regelwerk) with bounds

  • Requirement: The system shall store named machine rules with a value, a minimum and maximum bound, a check/format specification and responsibility flag, and shall apply these rules to validate machining data against the selected machine.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: The Regelwerk encodes machine-specific limits (e.g. permissible values) that guard NC generation from producing invalid programs.
  • Source: Punch.MAS_REGEL columns (MA_REGEL_NAME, MA_REGEL_TYP, MA_REGEL_WERT, MA_REGEL_WERT_MIN, MA_REGEL_WERT_MAX, MA_REGEL_PRUEF_INFO, MA_REGEL_FACTOR, MA_REGEL_FORMAT); Punch.STANZ_REGELN, Punch.REGEL_ALLG; gritx datenteil/te3_reg.gritx, te2_reg.gritx.
  • Verification: Analysis — trace a rule value through validation and confirm out-of-bounds machining data is rejected.

FR-TECH-008 — Machine rotation capability and cross-machine grouping

  • Requirement: The system shall record whether a machine can rotate a tool (C-axis) and shall expose machine group codes (GMT group, LTT group) so that geometry and technology can be shared across machines of the same group during nesting.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Nesting for multiple machines ([[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]]) needs to know which machines can rotate tools and which share technology grouping.
  • Source: tc_maschine/TcMaschinenSchluessel.h CanMachineRotate, GetGMTGroup, GetLttGroup, IsMachineInFavoriteList.
  • Verification: Test — query CanMachineRotate for a rotating and a non-rotating machine and confirm correct results.

FR-TECH-009 — Sheet loading-position strategy computation

  • Requirement: The system shall compute the sheet loading position for a machine/sheet from a selectable loading strategy (fixed value, centered, at grip, at clamp, more-punching, more-laser, from table, SheetMaster-Compact), returning the position of the left sheet edge in machine coordinates and reporting which strategies are admissible.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Automated loading requires a deterministic loading position derived from machine geometry and equipment; only automation-equipped machines use it.
  • Source: tc_maschine/TcBeladeStratege.h enum TeEinlegeStrategie (e_festwert … e_anSMCompact), getEinlegePosition, getMoeglicheStrategien, istStrategieMoeglich.
  • Verification: Test — for a machine with grip and clamp, compute loading positions for each admissible strategy and confirm they are consistent with work-area geometry.

FR-TECH-010 — Punch-tool record management (create / read / update / delete)

  • Requirement: Where the punching technology is licensed, the system shall maintain punch-tool records — identified by ident number, stamp number and cutting-edge number — carrying type, remark, min/max sheet thickness, up to six dimensional values, drawing references, punch contour and blocking/whisper flags, and shall support creating, reading, updating and deleting these records.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The tool catalog is the core of tc_wzg; punching cannot assign tools without maintainable tool records.
  • Source: tc_wzg/wzg_asm/tool_admin.hpp enum TeAction (e_actionRead/Update/Insert/Remove_Werkzeuge, readAndWriteTools, insertTool, removeTools); Punch.WZG3_DATEN columns (WZG3_IDENTNR, WZG3_TYPE, WZG3_STEMPELNR, WZG3_CUTTING_EDGE_NUMBER, WZG3_ZBLECH_MIN/MAX, WZG3_WERT_1..6, WZG3_ZEICHNUNG, WZG3_SPERREN, WZG3_WHISPER); gritx tec_wzg.gritx masks M_WzgErstellenStandardTyp, M_WzgParameter, M_WzgStandard, F_WzgParaIdentnummer.
  • Verification: Test — create a tool via the dialog, read it back, edit a value, delete it, and confirm each operation persists correctly.

FR-TECH-011 — Tool-type classification taxonomy

  • Requirement: The system shall classify each tool by a defined tool-type taxonomy (including round, rectangular, square, rectangular-with-radius, oblong-hole, special, centre-punch, laser, forming, embossing, banana, radius, triangular, slotting, marking, multi-cut, multi-shear/-bend, roller cutting/pinching/folding/beading/knurling/deburring, engraving, calibration, protection, push-out, thread-cutter, twist-drill, flow-drill) and by forming direction (up / down / none).
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Tool type governs which processing operations and which technology parameters apply; the taxonomy is fixed and shared across punch/laser/tube.
  • Source: tc_wzg/tool_base/TnTool.h namespace TnType::TeType, TnFormingDirection, TnDieType, TnCartridgeType, TnPunchNumber, TnCuttingEdgeNumber; Punch.WZG1_TYP (WZG1_TYPEN, WZG1_MULTITOOLABLE, WZG1_LITEABLE, WZG1_TOOLTYPEGROUP); Punch.WZG_DEF_TYP.
  • Verification: Inspection — confirm the tool-type enum values map to the WZG1_TYP / WZG_DEF_TYP catalog entries.

FR-TECH-012 — Multi-tool and multi-cut tool definition

  • Requirement: Where the punching technology is licensed, the system shall support defining multi-tools holding 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10 or 12 stamps at defined mounting positions (pitch-circle radius and keyway/nut angle per stamp) and multi-cut tools, including dynamic multi-tools.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Multi-tools and multi-cut increase punching productivity and require dedicated position/geometry data.
  • Source: tc_wzg/tool_base/TnTool.h namespace TnMultiToolType::TeMultiToolType, TyDynamicMultiToolPunchesByDynamicMultiToolId; tc_wzg/wzg_asm/werkzeug.hpp getMountingRadiusForMultiToolPunch, getMountingAngleForMultiToolPunch; Punch.WZG3_DATEN WZG3_MULTI, WZG3_PITCH_CIRCLE_RADIUS, WZG3_KEYWAY_ANGLE; gritx tec_wzg.gritx masks M_WzgErstellenMultitoolTyp, M_WzgMultitoolDynamisch, F_WzgErstellenMultiCut, M_WzgMulticut; tc_wzg/wzg_tech/wzg_util/TcMultiCutCheck.*.
  • Verification: Demonstration — create a 4-position multi-tool and confirm each stamp's position is stored.

FR-TECH-013 — Die (Matrize) definition and type

  • Requirement: Where the punching technology is licensed, the system shall maintain die definitions and their die type (standard, slanted, integrated-flattening), including the die matrix drawing reference and the assignment of a die to a tool's cutting edge.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Correct die selection is required for punching quality and clearance; die type affects processing.
  • Source: tc_wzg/tool_base/TnTool.h namespace TnDieType; Punch.DIE (DIE_KEY, DIE_TYPE, DIE_TEXT); Punch.LASERMATRIZE; Punch.WZG_TECHDATA WTD_ZEICHNUNG_MATRIZE; Punch.WZG3_DATEN WZG3_DIE_TYPE, WZG3_ACTIVE_OR_DESCENDING_DIE; gritx tec_wzg.gritx mask M_DieTypeSelection.
  • Verification: Test — assign each die type to a tool and confirm the die type is persisted and read back.

FR-TECH-014 — Thread-former tools and material/thickness assignment

  • Requirement: Where thread forming/cutting is licensed, the system shall maintain thread-former tools (thread size, ratio, pitch, direction, tip, thread type) and their assignment to material and sheet-thickness ranges.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Thread forming requires matching a former to material and thickness; this is a distinct, edition-dependent tool family.
  • Source: tc_wzg/wzg_asm/tool_admin.hpp actions e_actionRead/Update/Insert/Remove/Copy_Gewindeformer, _GewindeWsMas, _GewindeWsDicke; Punch.GEWINDEFORMER (GF_GEWINDE, GF_UEBERSETZUNG, GF_STEIGUNG, GF_DREHRICHTUNG, GF_SPITZE, GF_GEWINDETYP); Punch.GEWINDE_WS_MAS, Punch.GEWINDE_WS_DICKE; gritx tec_wzg.gritx masks F_WzgErstellenGewinde, M_Wzg_GewindeParameter, M_GewindeFormerDatenScroll, tc_bof/GewindeFormDaten.gritx.
  • Verification: Demonstration — define a thread former and assign it to a material/thickness, then confirm it is offered for that combination.

FR-TECH-015 — Per-tool technology parameters

  • Requirement: The system shall store per-tool technology parameters — including minimum radius, X/Y offset, chamfer height, stamp-break-control and typed parameter sets — separately from the tool geometry, keyed by tool ident, stamp number and cutting-edge number.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Technology parameters tune how a tool is applied and vary independently of the physical tool; they feed the punch processor.
  • Source: Punch.WZG_TECHDATA (WTD_MIN_RADIUS, WTD_VERSATZ_DX/DY, WTD_ZEICHNUNG_MATRIZE); Punch.WZG_TECHPARAM, Punch.WZG_TECHPARAMSET; Punch.WZG3_DATEN WZG3_CHAMFER_HEIGHT, WZG3_STEMPELBRUCHKONTROLLE; tc_wzg/wzg_tech/wzg_util/TcWzgTechDataParameterMapper.*, TcParameterMapper.*.
  • Verification: Test — set a tool offset and min radius and confirm the punch processor reads the stored values.

FR-TECH-016 — Named tool lists

  • Requirement: The system shall allow tools to be grouped into named tool lists and shall support creating, reading, copying, renaming and deleting those lists.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Tool lists model the tool magazine / a machine's loaded tool set and constrain automatic tool assignment.
  • Source: tc_wzg/wzg_asm/tool_admin.hpp (readToolLists, insertToolList, removeToolList, removeWerkzeugListe, m_toolsFromList); gritx tec_wzg.gritx masks M_WzgErstellenListe, M_WzgErstellenListeName, M_WzgListeKopieren, M_WzgListeUmbenennen, F_Wzg_VerwendeteWerkzeuge.
  • Verification: Demonstration — create a tool list, add tools, rename and delete it.

FR-TECH-017 — Tool import and export (XML)

  • Requirement: The system shall import tool definitions from a tool XML file into the tool database and shall export tool data to file, including cancelling an in-progress import.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Tools are exchanged between systems and pre-provisioned; XML import/export avoids manual re-entry. See [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]].
  • Source: tc_wzg/wzg_tech/wzg_imp/TcWzgImport.h (imports tool XML into WZG3, holds data in TcWzgEinzel/TcWzgDaten), TcWzgExport.*, TcWzgXmlKeys.h; tc_wzg/wzg_asm/werkzeug.hpp getWzgImport, cancelImport; gritx tec_wzg.gritx masks M_WzgImportWzgDaten, M_WzgExportWzgDaten.
  • Verification: Test — import a tool XML file and confirm the tools appear in the catalog; export and re-import round-trip.

FR-TECH-018 — Central/networked tool management connection and availability

  • Requirement: Where the integrated/central tool-management interface (ITM / SW06) is available, the system shall connect to it, report the connection state, and compute tool availability so the user is informed which tools are present for the current machine/material context.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Networked tool management provides live tool availability; the connection is optional/licence-gated.
  • Source: tc_wzg/wzg_tech/wzg_util/TcSW06Adapter.*, TcSW06TopsWerkzeug.*, TcSW06ExceptionHandler.*, TcItmConnectionStateChangedEventHandler.h ("Management (visualization) of ITM connection state"), TcToolAvailabilityResult.*, TcToolAvailabilityCalculated/FailedEventHandler.*; tc_wzg/wzg_tech/wzg_sort/TcToolAvailabilityTest.*.
  • Verification: Demonstration — with ITM connected, confirm availability status is shown; with it disconnected, confirm the state is reported.

FR-TECH-019 — Tool filtering and sorting for assignment

  • Requirement: The system shall filter and sort the tool inventory by criteria including tool type, sheet-thickness range, dimensions, enclosing-circle, whisper flag, blocking flag, multi-tool capability, long-tool flag, active-die and tool-availability, to support automatic and manual tool selection.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Automatic tool assignment must narrow a large catalog to admissible candidates for a given contour/machine.
  • Source: tc_wzg/wzg_tech/wzg_sort/ (TcWzgFilter.*, TcBlechdickeTest, TcTypenTest, TcMassN*Test, TcHuellkreisGrobTest, TcWhispertoolTest, TcSperrTest, TcMTTest, TcLangesWzgTest, TcActiveDieTest, TcToolAvailabilityTest, wzg_sort.*); sort orders under wzg_tech/wzg_util/TsSort*.
  • Verification: Test — apply a thickness+type filter and confirm only admissible tools are returned.

FR-TECH-020 — Material (Werkstoff) master data

  • Requirement: The system shall maintain material (Werkstoff) master records carrying physical and technological properties — specific weight, tensile strength (min/max, Rm), yield strength Rp, E-modulus, hardness, thermal conductivity, electrical resistance coefficient, melting and boiling temperature, Brewster reflection angle, magnetic percentage of steel, specific embossing force and springback — together with international designations (DIN/EN, material number, ASTM, JIS).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Material properties drive laser cutting parameters, punching force, weight/cost estimation and part behaviour; they are shared across all processes and editions.
  • Source: tc_materialVerwaltung/material.h (Material), TcWerkstoff.h (getters for Zugfestigkeit, SpezGewicht, EModul, StreckgrenzeRp, MagneticPercentage, SpezPraegeKraft, Rueckfederung, DIN/ASTM/JIS/WerkstoffNr); Common.MAT_MATERIAL columns (WS_SPEZ_GEWICHT, WS_RM, WS_RP, WS_E_MODUL, WS_HAERTE, WS_WAERMELEITFAEHIGKEIT, WS_SCHMELZTEMP, WS_MAGNETIC_PERC_OF_STEEL, WS_SPEZ_PRAEGE_KRAFT, WS_RUECKFEDERUNG, WS_DIN, WS_EURONORM, WS_NUMMER, WS_ASTM, WS_JIS).
  • Verification: Test — create a material, set its properties, and confirm they are returned by TcWerkstoff getters.

FR-TECH-021 — Raw-material (thickness) definition

  • Requirement: The system shall maintain raw-material records that pair a material with a thickness and additional processing attributes — foil type, surface coating, scratch-free/high-quality flag, tool clearance (min/opt/max), load tolerances and optical reflection — and shall support looking up the thickness for a given raw-material name.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Processing is selected by material + thickness; foil/coating and tool clearance affect cutting and punching.
  • Source: Common.MAT_RAWMATERIAL columns (RM_WS_ID, RM_THICKNESS, RM_NAME, RM_FOIL, RM_COATING, RM_SCRATCHFREE, RM_TOOLCLEARANCEMIN/OPT/MAX, RM_MIN/MAX_LOAD_TOL, RM_OPTICAL_REFLECTION); material.h LookupRawMaterialThickness, enum TeFolienArt, enum TeVerzinkungsArt; TcMaterialDaten.h (GetBlechdicke, GetWerkstoffIndex).
  • Verification: Test — define a raw material with foil and thickness and confirm LookupRawMaterialThickness returns the correct value.

FR-TECH-022 — Sheet / stock (Lagergut) inventory definition

  • Requirement: The system shall maintain sheet/stock items that reference a raw material and carry concrete dimensions (X/Y), a remainder-sheet flag, grain direction, storage location, geometry reference and net quantity, so that nesting and part management can select real stock.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Nesting places parts on concrete sheets/remnants; the stock catalog links abstract raw material to physical inventory.
  • Source: Common.MAT_SHEET columns (MAT_LAGERGUTID, MAT_RM_NAME, MAT_ABMESS_X/Y, MAT_IS_REMAINDER_SHEET, MAT_GRAIN_DIRECTION, MAT_STORAGE_LOCATION, MAT_GEO_DIRECTORY/NAME, MAT_NET_QUANTITY); material.h (getLagergutbez, t_lagergutBez); TcMaterialDaten.h (getLagergut, getMaterialSize).
  • Verification: Demonstration — create a remnant sheet with dimensions and confirm it is selectable in nesting.

FR-TECH-023 — Material group and pricing

  • Requirement: The system shall organize materials into material groups carrying a group name, abbreviation and price-per-kilogram with currency, so that mass-based cost can be derived from a material's specific weight.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Cost/weight reporting relies on group pricing; it is a convenience for costing rather than a processing prerequisite.
  • Source: Common.MAT_GRP columns (MGRP_GROUPNAME, MGRP_ABBREV, MGRP_PRICE_PER_KG, MGRP_PRICE_CURRENCY); Common.MAT_MATERIAL WS_MGRP_ID; material.h/TcWerkstoff computeMass, getSpezGewicht.
  • Verification: Analysis — confirm price-per-kg and specific weight combine to a mass-based cost.

FR-TECH-024 — Allowed machining type per material

  • Requirement: The system shall record, per material, which machining processes are permitted (laser, water-jet, punching, bending, plasma) as a bit-mask and shall restrict process/technology selection to the permitted set.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Not every material may be processed by every technology; the flag prevents invalid process assignment.

FR-TECH-025 — Material aliases and technology mapping

  • Requirement: The system shall maintain material aliases and mappings that resolve a material/raw-material to the correct laser technology table, threading and bend-factor data (LTT alias, thread alias, bend-factor alias) and to raw-material→LTT associations.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The same physical material may be named differently across technology tables; aliases keep the technology lookup consistent.
  • Source: Common.MAT_MATERIAL (WS_LTT_ALIAS, WS_THREAD_ALIAS, WS_BEND_FACTOR_ALIAS, WS_ABBREV); TcWerkstoff.h (getLttAlias, getThreadingAlias, getBendAbkfAlias); Common.MAT_ALIAS, Common.MAT_RAWMAT_MAP_LTT; Punch.LASERTECHNO_ALIAS.

FR-TECH-026 — Laser technology tables (LTT) — cutting parameters

  • Requirement: Where laser cutting is licensed, the system shall maintain laser technology tables keyed by machine type, laser power, material and thickness, storing cutting parameters — lens focal length, nozzle type, cutting-head number, gas type and pressure, focus position, feed rate, acceleration, corner-dwell time and per-contour-size (large/medium/small) power/feed/gas parameters.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The LTT is the parameter backbone of laser cutting quality and speed for each material/thickness/machine combination.
  • Source: Punch.LASERTECHNO / Laser.LASERTECHNO columns (LT_ALL_MASCH_TYP, LT_ALL_LASER_LSTG, LT_ALL_MATERIAL, LT_ALL_MAT_DICKE, LT_ALL_LINSENBRENNW, LT_ALL_DUESENTYP, LT_ALL_SCHNEIDKOPFNR, LT_SCH_FOKUSLAGE, LT_SCH_GASART, LT_SCH_GR_*/MI_* …); Punch.LASERTAB, LASERTECHNO2, LASERTECHNOBC, LASERTOPS; Common.LaserheadCompatibility (HeadMounted, HeadCompatible).
  • Verification: Test — select a material/thickness/machine and confirm the matching LTT parameters are returned.

FR-TECH-027 — LTT process definitions (cut / pierce / mark / centre-punch / micro-weld / de-foil)

  • Requirement: Where laser cutting is licensed, the system shall let the user define and edit laser technology per process step — contour cutting (incl. LLS/sensor variants), piercing, marking (Kennzeichnen), centre-punching (Körnen), micro-welding, and foil vaporization (Abdampfen) — including solid-state/YAG-laser variants of these steps.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Each laser process step has distinct parameters; users must edit them per step and laser source.
  • Source: gritx datenteil/te3_ltt.gritx masks M_TECHNO_LTT_SCHNEIDEN_ALLG/KONTUR/LLS/SENSOR/YAG, M_TECHNO_LTT_EINSTECHEN(_PCS/_YAG), M_TECHNO_LTT_KENNZEICHNEN(_YAG), M_TECHNO_LTT_KOERNEN(_YAG), M_TECHNO_LTT_MICRO_WELD(_YAG), M_TECHNO_LTT_ABDAMPFEN(_YAG), M_TECHNO_LTT_ALLG_LASER/BESCHL/ZEITEN/KOPF.

FR-TECH-028 — LTT management and replacement in technology

  • Requirement: Where laser cutting is licensed, the system shall support creating, copying, deleting and selecting laser technology tables, and shall support replacing/exchanging the LTT referenced by an existing part or nest technology, warning when a referenced material or rule set is missing.

FR-TECH-029 — AutoCut configuration

  • Requirement: Where the AutoCut feature is licensed, the system shall maintain named AutoCut configurations as typed key/value parameter sets (long, double, string, with unit) that steer automatic processing for punch, laser, combi and slug-removal (Ausschleusen), and shall read, edit and save these configurations.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: AutoCut automates technology assignment; its behaviour is parameterized by editable configuration records.
  • Source: Punch.AUTOCUT_KONFIG columns (ACK_ID, ACK_KONFIG, ACK_TYP, ACK_LONG, ACK_DOUBLE, ACK_CHAR, ACK_EINHEIT); gritx tec_acut.gritx masks M_AutoCutKonfiguration, F_AutoCutKonfig_Allg/Punch/Laser/Ausschl, FUNC_ACUT_ReadParameter/SaveParameter/SetKonfig, FUNC_ACUT_WzgAuswahl, F_AutoRutschenPruef.
  • Verification: Demonstration — change an AutoCut parameter, save, reopen and confirm the value persisted.

FR-TECH-030 — Micro-joint (Steg) technology definitions

  • Requirement: Where laser cutting is licensed, the system shall maintain machine- and material-specific micro-joint (Steg) definitions — bridge type, range, start number, step width, count, position and default state — used to hold parts in the skeleton.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Micro-joints prevent part tip-up and are machine/material dependent; they must be catalogued to be applied during processing.
  • Source: Laser.MAS_STEGE columns (MST_MANAME, MST_MATYP, MST_BEREICH, MST_STARTNR, MST_SCHRITTWEITE, MST_ANZAHL, MST_ART, MST_DEFAULTZUSTAND); Laser.MAS_STEGTYPEN, Laser.STEG_TYP, Laser.STEG_WERKSTOFF.
  • Verification: Analysis — confirm a defined Steg type is offered for its machine/material and applied to a contour.

FR-TECH-031 — Tube technology and profile catalog

  • Requirement: Where tube/profile machining is licensed, the system shall maintain tube technology tables, a tube-material catalog (dimensions, tensile strength, specific weight, cross-section type) and a tube-profile catalog (profile geometry), together with tube guide/chuck definitions.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Tube machining ([[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]]) needs its own material, profile and fixturing master data distinct from flat-sheet data.
  • Source: Tube.TUBETECH, Tube.MAT_ROHR (MAT_ROHR_TYP, MAT_ROHR_ABMESS_X/Y/Z/R1/R2/D/L, MAT_ROHR_ZUGFEST, MAT_ROHR_SPEZ_GEW), Tube.PROFILTAB (PRF_TYP, PRF_STARTPKT*, PRF_ENDPKT*, PRF_MITTELPKT*, PRF_BOGENRICHTUNG), Tube.ROHRFUEHRUNGEN, Tube.ROHRFUTTER(_MAS); gritx roh_tech.gritx, roh_prfvrw.gritx mask M_RohrProfilMaterialAuswahl.
  • Verification: Demonstration — define a tube profile and material and confirm they are selectable for a tube job.

5. External Interfaces (area-specific)

  • Relational master database (SQL Server / ODBC). All machine, tool, material and technology master data lives in the Punch, Laser, Tube and Common schemas (Databases/SQLServerDatabase/Tables). Key tables: MASCHINE, MAS_ALLG, MAS_REGEL, STEUERUNGSVERSIONEN, WZG3_DATEN, WZG1_TYP, WZG_TECHDATA/PARAM, DIE, GEWINDEFORMER, KASSETTE, MAT_MATERIAL, MAT_RAWMATERIAL, MAT_SHEET, MAT_GRP, LASERTECHNO(2/BC), LASERTAB, AUTOCUT_KONFIG, MAS_STEGE, TUBETECH, PROFILTAB, MAT_ROHR. Dialogs reach the tables through the gritx MMK-interpreter mini_sql(...) construct. See [[30 - External Interface Requirements]].
  • Tool XML files. Tool import/export exchanges tool definitions as XML (TcWzgImport/TcWzgExport, TcWzgXmlKeys). See [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]].
  • Central tool management (ITM / SW06). A .NET business-model wrapper (Trumpf_SW06_Businessmodel_Common_Interface_Wrapper) connects to networked tool management for live tool availability (TcSW06Adapter, TcItmConnectionStateChangedEventHandler).
  • Text/localization tables. Material, tool, machine and rule labels are localized via text-number references (e.g. WZG3_BEMERK_TEXTNR, MA_REGEL_TEXTNR, Text.COMMON_TEXT).
  • Machines/controllers (downstream). The master data parameterizes NC output for the physical machines/controllers; NC generation is specified in [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]].

6. Assumptions & Constraints

  • Legacy German domain model. Column, class and mask names are German (Werkzeug, Werkstoff, Blechdicke, Steg, Beladen …); English requirement text keeps the original term in parentheses. See [[04 - Domain Model & Glossary]] and [[50 - Constraints & Assumptions]].
  • Schema-per-technology. The same logical table (e.g. MAS_ALLG, LASERTECHNO, MASCHINE) is replicated across the Punch, Laser and Tube schemas; a given edition operates on its schema. Material master (MAT_*) and units live in the shared Common schema.
  • Metric/inch duality. Tool and material measures exist in metric and inch forms (Punch.WZG_DEF_MASS WDM_UNIT_METRIC/INCH, MAT_RAWMATERIAL.RM_ISITINCH, Common.UNITS/SI_UNITS/INCHFRACTIONS); a nightly inch-refresh job (DB_Port_SqlSrv_InchRefresh_*) maintains derived inch data. (inferred)
  • Edition/licence gating. Punch-specific (dies, multi-tools, cassettes, thread formers), laser-specific (LTT, Steg), tube-specific and ITM/AutoCut capabilities are only present in the corresponding editions; the ini-file tree enumerates them. Priorities marked Conditional reflect this.
  • Master data precedes processing. The processors ([[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]], [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]], [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]]) assume valid, consistent master data; consistency checking exists (TiConsistencyChecker) but its full rule set was not read.
  • Lock/visibility state. Master records carry visibility and lock-state flags (*_VISIBLE, *_LOCKSTATE) implying a governance/read-only mechanism whose exact semantics were not fully traced. (inferred)

7. Open Questions & Ambiguities

  • tc_mtl scope. tc_mtl (bitmap material, bitmap.*, bitkoll = collision, ztstore, zt_mtl) appears to be a bit-map representation of the sheet/skeleton used for nesting collision detection, not material master data. It was assigned to this area by name but likely belongs to [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]]. Flagged for the nesting analyst to confirm ownership.
  • tc_tec/tec_tech/taf_la (Resttafel / fly-cut / circle-correction). Remaining-sheet management (TcResttafelList/Info), fly-cut calculation (TcFlyCutCalculator) and circle correction (TcCircleCorrection) read technology settings here but are really laser/NC processing concerns; the boundary with [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]] and [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]] is unclear.
  • Bend tool tables. The Bend.WZG_* tables (Gesenk, Adapter, Sauger, Greifzange …) are an extensive parallel tool model for bending; they are owned by [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]] and are intentionally excluded here.
  • MAT_DETECTOR, AXIS_MODE_DATA/POSITION, VOREINSTELLUNG. These Punch/Laser tables clearly configure machine/material behaviour but their exact roles (material detector sensor mapping? axis-mode presets? default presets?) could not be determined from the schema alone.
  • Consistency rules. The concrete validation rules enforced by TiConsistencyChecker and the machine Regelwerk (MAS_REGEL types) were not enumerated; only the mechanism is evidenced.
  • Exact LTT parameter semantics. LASERTECHNO has hundreds of columns (GR/MI/KL contour-size variants, NOR/RED normal/reduced power); the precise meaning of every parameter and how big/medium/small contour thresholds are chosen was not fully reverse-engineered.
  • WZG3_UsedByApplicationType. The tool's application-type bit-mask (laser2d/kombi/tube/laser3d combinations, TnUsedByApplicationType) governs cross-technology tool sharing; the rules for how it is set and enforced were not traced.
