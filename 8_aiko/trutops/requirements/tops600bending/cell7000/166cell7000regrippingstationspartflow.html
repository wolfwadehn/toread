Cell7000 Detail — Regripping, Stations & Part Flow

Reverse-engineered requirement draft — derived from static analysis of legacy source, NOT an authoritative specification. Treat SHALL statements as reconstructed intent. Tier-2 detail refining [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]]; not added to the 603-item register.

1. Purpose & Scope

This document specifies the material-handling data model of the automated bending cell: regripping (Umgreifen) rules and the regrip console, the turning/flip station (Wendestation), the part-removal / unload station (Entnahmestation) with its pallet, process parameters, gripper collision geometry and suction-cup occupancy, interleaving layers (Zwischenlage), pallets (Paletten) and the conveyor (Foerderer). Together these define how a finished part is re-oriented, removed, measured, stacked and conveyed out of the cell around the bending program.

Provenance & evidence note. No cluster-D table contains a literal "Cell7000"/"BendMaster"; the Cell 7000 relationship is structural/inferred — suction-cup regrip consoles, a flip station, a camera/thickness-measuring unload station and system pallets are characteristic of a high-automation cell. Most tables are SSMA-migrated (T600_V100.*); notably WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_PALETTE and WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_GRIPPER_PARAMETER carry no MS_SSMA_SOURCE (native/newer, likely Cell-7000-era additions). Unlike other clusters, several real foreign keys exist here (noted per requirement). These tables hold equipment geometry and handling parameters, not bend NC; they constrain the motion/handling planning the cell controller executes.

2. Capability Overview

  • Define regripping rules (Regelwerk) by category (suction / tool-rest), part width/length/weight, required suction-cup count and whether laying the part on a tool is allowed; plus global regrip parameters.
  • Model a regrip console (Umgreifkonsole) — a vacuum-suction beam with geometry, mounting side/position, load limit, vacuum timings, pressure monitoring, a turning-station reference, travel speed and synchronous-travel capability — and its per-suction-cup occupancy.
  • Model a turning/flip station (Wendestation) that re-orients a part, with placement, orientation, deposit-surface geometry and an identification method.
  • Model a part-removal / unload station (Entnahmestation): station geometry, fill/load limits, pallet link and part-stop, its pallet (segmented, tilted, with safety clearances), its process parameters (transfer table, camera part-height, sheet-thickness measurement, separating nozzles, system pallet) and its gripper collision geometry (flat/tilted at 0°/90°), plus per-suction-cup occupancy.
  • Model interleaving layers (Zwischenlage), general pallets (Paletten) and a conveyor (Foerderer).

3. Domain Entities

4. Functional Requirements

FR-CELL-HDL-001 — Regripping rule set

  • Requirement: The system SHALL hold regripping rules categorised by kind (e.g. suction, tool-rest), parametrised by part width, length and weight, giving the required number of suction cups and whether laying the part onto a tool is permitted.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Whether and how a part can be safely re-gripped depends on its size/weight and on cup count; these rules are preconditions the sequence planner must respect.
  • Source: Databases/SQLServerDatabase/Tables/Bend.UMGREIFEN_REGELWERK.sql (REGELWERK_KATEGORIE "Sauger, Werkzeugauflage", TEIL_BREITE, TEIL_LAENGE, TEIL_GEWICHT, SAUGER_ANZ, AUFLEGEN_WKZ "0: erlaubt, 1 nicht erlaubt").
  • Verification: Analysis.

FR-CELL-HDL-002 — Global regripping parameters

  • Requirement: The system SHALL maintain a set of scalar regripping parameters keyed by id and identified by a source-code name.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Regrip behaviour is tuned by named global parameters shared across the regrip logic.
  • Source: Bend.UMGREIFEN_PARAMETER.sql (PARAMETER_NUMMER, Bezeichnung "name des parameters zur identifikation im sourcecode", Wert).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-003 — Regrip console master

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model a regrip console with its geometry, mounting side (left/right) and mounting kind (table vs. regrip unit), installation position, maximum load, minimum neighbour distance, suction-position limits in Y, vacuum timings, pressure monitoring, a turning-station reference, travel speed, and whether it can travel synchronously with the regrip unit.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The console is the surface onto which the part is laid to re-grip; its geometry, load limit and timings drive collision-free regrip planning.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_UMGREIFKONSOLE.sql (LageID, MontageID, Laenge/Breite/Hoehe, EinbaupositionY/Z, BelastungMax, MinAbstand, MinPosSaugerInY/MaxPosSaugerInY, Ansaugzeit, AusblaszeitKeinVakuum/AusblaszeitKeinHalten, Druckueberwachung, Wendestation, Geschwindigkeit, SynchronVerfahren).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-004 — Regrip console suction-cup occupancy

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model the per-suction-cup occupancy of a regrip console — each cup's number, group, X/Y position, angle, Z offset, holder/profile geometry, angle intervals and travel range, arm kinematic type (sliding / rotatable / rotatable-with-telescope), arm-length range, and whether the arm is mounted.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The cup layout and arm kinematics on the console determine where the part is supported during regrip and must be collision-checked.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_UMGREIFBELEGUNG.sql (Saugernummer, Gruppennummer, PositionX/Y, Winkellage, SaugerversatzZ, Winkel{Min,Max}, Winkel2{Min,Max}, WinkelSchrittweite, Armlaenge{,Min,Max,Schrittweite}, SaugarmTyp, Montiert; FK ID → WZG_UMGREIFKONSOLE.ID ON DELETE CASCADE).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-005 — Turning / flip station

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model a turning/flip station with its placement (position + roll/pitch/yaw), zero-point offsets, deposit-surface geometry, tab heights, start angle, an identification method and drawing references for fixed, rotating and cylinder parts.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Re-orienting a part (e.g. flipping) between bends requires a physical flip station whose geometry the planner must know.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_WENDESTATION.sql (Position{X,Y,Z}, Orientierung{X,Y,Z} "(Roll,Pitch,Yaw)", Nullpunktversatz{X,Y,Z}, AbstandAblageflaeche{X,Z}, AbstandWendeachseZ, RadiusAblageflaeche, Laschenhoehe{Oben,Unten}, Startwinkel, Identifizierung, Zeichnung{Fix,Rot,Zylinder}). Referenced by WZG_UMGREIFKONSOLE.Wendestation (soft, by name).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-006 — Unload / part-removal station master

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model a part-removal (unload) station with its geometry, maximum fill height and load weight, drop height (bin case), floor offset, side-wall presence/height, a pallet-type link, and a part-stop offset and direction in X and Y.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Finished parts are deposited at an unload station; its capacity, geometry and part-stop define where and how parts are placed.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION.sql (BeschreibungID "Palettentyp", Laenge/Breite/Hoehe, MaxFuellhoehe, MaxLadegewicht, Abwurfhoehe, OffsetBoden, Seitenwand/HoeheSeitenwand, TeilanschlagVersatzInX/Y, TeilanschlagInX/YRichtung, WinkelZ). Parent of the ENTNAHMESTATION family (FKs, cascade).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-007 — Unload-station pallet (segmented, tilted)

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model the pallet placed on an unload station, including its box position/dimensions, wall and segment-wall thicknesses, segment grid (Fächer in X and Y), tilt angles about X and Y, a 3D model and gripper-to-pallet safety clearances; and SHALL constrain these values (positive dimensions, ≥1 segments, tilt within ±180°, non-negative clearances, Typ free of path separators).
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Placing parts into a segmented/tilted pallet without collision requires the pallet's exact geometry and safety clearances.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_PALETTE.sql (Entnahmebox{Position,Dimension}{X,Y,Z}, PalettenWandStaerke, PalettenSegmentWandStaerke, PalettenSegmentDimension{X,Y}, AnzahlFaecher{X,Y}, Kippwinkel{X,Y}, Modell3D, SicherheitsabstandGreiferPaletten{X,Y}; CHECK constraints as stated; FK ID → WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION.ID ON DELETE CASCADE; no MS_SSMA_SOURCE).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-008 — Unload-station process parameters (transfer table, camera, measurement, nozzles)

  • Requirement: The system SHALL hold the unload station's process parameters, including the transfer-table (Übergabetisch) origin and dimensions, camera part-height, system-pallet geometry, extraction-box and rotation-point positions, separating-nozzle geometry and spacing, a sheet-thickness-measurement flag with three measuring probes and maximum sheet thickness, and the selected system pallet.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: An automated, vision- and measurement-equipped unload station needs these process parameters to transfer, recognise, measure and separate parts.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_PARAMETER.sql (Uebergabetisch*, TeilhoeheUeberKamera, Palette*, Entnahmebox*, Rotationspunkt*, Trennduesen*/Trennduesenabstand/VertikalHubVorTrennduesen, FlagBlechdickenmessung, Messtaster{1,2,3}{X,Y}, MesstasterDurchmesser, MaxBlechdicke, EntnahmePalette default 'sloped_2_2'; FKs to WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION and WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_PALETTE).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-009 — Unload-station gripper collision geometry (flat/tilted, 0°/90°)

  • Requirement: The system SHALL hold the collision/kinematic geometry of the gripper used at the unload station for flat and tilted poses at 0° and 90° rotation — gripper dimensions, gripper-to-mount distances, gripper-pallet safety clearances, axis-aligned bounding boxes, base/suction-plate order numbers, a 90°-rotated 3D model, virtual tilt-point translations and tilt angles (alpha/beta/gamma) — and SHALL constrain the tilt angles to ±360°.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Picking parts from a segmented/sloped pallet at different orientations requires per-pose gripper geometry for collision checking.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_GRIPPER_PARAMETER.sql (GreiferDimension{0,90,Gekippt…}, AbstandGreiferAufnahme…, SicherheitsabstandGreiferPaletten{X,Y}, BoundingBox…, Bestellnummer{Basisplatte,Saugplatte}, Skizze3DGedreht90Grad, TransVirtKipppkt…, KippWinkel{Alpha,Beta,Gamma}; FK Saugertyp → WZG_SAUGER_TYP.Typ; no MS_SSMA_SOURCE).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-010 — Unload-station suction-cup occupancy (dual candidate positions)

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model the per-suction-cup occupancy of an unload station, carrying two candidate positions per cup with an active-position selector, plus a linear displacement range and angle range.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The unload gripper's cup layout may switch between two positions for different parts; both must be modelled and one selected.
  • Source: Bend.WZG_ENTNAHMESTATIONBELEGUNG.sql (Position{X,Y,Z}1, Position{X,Y,Z}2, AktivePosition, Verschiebung{,Min,Max,Schrittweite}, Winkel{Min,Max,Schrittweite}; FK ID → WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION.ID ON DELETE CASCADE). Working copy: Bend.WZG_TMP_ENTNAHMESTATIONBELEGUNG (no FK, transient).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-011 — Interleaving layer (separator sheet)

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model an interleaving layer — its designation, L×W×H and a drawing (top-view GEO or 3D-SAT) — for placement between stacked finished parts.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Separator sheets protect stacked parts; the cell picks and places them by their defined geometry.
  • Source: Bend.ZWISCHENLAGE.sql (Typ, Laenge/Breite/Hoehe, SkizzeDetail). Selected via Bend.ROBOTERPROZESSDATEN.INTERLAYER* (see [[16.4 - Cell7000 — Robot, Process & Motion Data]]).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-012 — General pallets

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model general pallets/bins (independent of a specific unload station) with geometry, fill/load limits, drop height, floor offset, side-wall data, a pallet-type number and an identification method.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: A reusable pallet/bin master supports stacking configurations shared across stations.
  • Source: Bend.PALETTEN.sql (BeschreibungID "Palettentyp", Laenge/Breite/Hoehe, MaxFuellhoehe, MaxLadegewicht, Abwurfhoehe, OffsetBoden, Seitenwand/HoeheSeitenwand, Identifizierung, SkizzeDetail).
  • Verification: Inspection.

FR-CELL-HDL-013 — Conveyor

  • Requirement: The system SHALL model a conveyor that transports finished parts out of the cell, with its placement, an identification method, a 3D sketch and X/Y/Z deposit offsets relative to the zero point.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Conveyor-based part removal is an alternative to pallet stacking and must be placed for hand-off planning.
  • Source: Bend.FOERDERER.sql (Position{X,Y,Z}, Identifizierung, Skizze3D, VersatzIn{X,Y,Z}). Data-quality caveat: the PositionX/Y/Z column↔description mapping is cross-wired in the source metadata (transcribed verbatim; column names govern).
  • Verification: Inspection.

5. External Interfaces (area-specific)

  • ToPs 600 bend database — UMGREIFEN_*, WZG_UMGREIFKONSOLE/WZG_UMGREIFBELEGUNG, WZG_WENDESTATION, WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION*, ZWISCHENLAGE, PALETTEN, FOERDERER. See [[16.7 - Cell7000 — Bend Automation Data Model (Reference)]].
  • Robot process defaults — station/conveyor/interlayer names in Bend.ROBOTERPROZESSDATEN reference these masters (see [[16.4 - Cell7000 — Robot, Process & Motion Data]]).
  • Suction-cup type master — WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_GRIPPER_PARAMETER.Saugertyp → WZG_SAUGER_TYP.Typ (see [[16.5 - Cell7000 — Gripper & Handling Tooling]]).
  • Palletising / part flow (system-level) — the cell's stacking/handling parallels the punch/laser palletising in [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]] but is bend-specific and lives in the Bend schema.

6. Assumptions & Constraints

  • A1. These tables hold equipment geometry and handling parameters, not bend NC; they constrain the motion/handling planning executed by the cell controller. (inferred)
  • A2. The two "console + occupancy" structures are parallel: regrip (WZG_UMGREIFKONSOLE 1—N WZG_UMGREIFBELEGUNG) and unload (WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION 1—N WZG_ENTNAHMESTATIONBELEGUNG, plus parameter/pallet/gripper-geometry children).
  • C1. WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_PALETTE and WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_GRIPPER_PARAMETER are native (no MS_SSMA_SOURCE) — likely newer Cell-7000-era additions. (inferred)
  • C2. Cell 7000 mapping is structural; no Typ value in these tables is tagged to the Cell 7000. (inferred)

7. Open Questions & Ambiguities

  1. Cell 7000 mapping is implicit. Which station Typ values correspond specifically to the TruBend Cell 7000 vs. earlier cells is not disambiguated in the schema.
  2. Bend pallet-type lookup. BeschreibungID ("Palettentyp") has no Bend.PALETTENTYP table (only Laser.PALETTENTYP/Punch.PALETTENTYP exist); where the Bend pallet-type enumeration is defined is unresolved.
  3. FOERDERER position column/description mismatch — X/Y/Z are cross-wired in the metadata; confirm whether the bug is in the description or the column usage.
  4. WZG_TMP_ENTNAHMESTATIONBELEGUNG lifecycle — when the temp occupancy is authoritative during a running job.
  5. Native (non-SSMA) tables — confirm WZG_ENTNAHMESTATION_PALETTE/_GRIPPER_PARAMETER are Cell-7000-era additions and whether they apply only to newer cells.
