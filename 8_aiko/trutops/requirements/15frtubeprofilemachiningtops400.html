FR — Tube & Profile Machining

Reverse-engineered requirement draft — derived from static analysis of legacy source, NOT an authoritative specification. Treat SHALL statements as reconstructed intent.

1. Purpose & Scope

This document specifies the functional requirements for Tube & Profile Machining — the CAM area of the TruTops CAD/CAM suite that programs laser cutting of tubes, pipes and open profiles on rotary/tube laser machines (TRUMPF TruLaser Tube / Tubematic class; historically ToPs Tube built on the ToPs 400 laser platform).

The area covers: interpreting a 3D tube part model and its cross-section (profile); switching between the 3D (Raum) representation and the flat unrolled (Abwicklung) representation; assigning machining ("cases", Fälle) to the tube — planar cuts, perpendicular cuts, tube-end/miter cuts, insert (Einsatz), mount (Aufsetzen) and through-penetration (Durchstecken); corner treatment, lead-in/lead-out, weld-seam positioning; tube laser technology and NC-cycle assignment; guiding/supporting the tube during cut (chuck, guides, bridges, finished-part supports); collision checking; machining-sequence optimization along the tube; measuring cycles; NC/APD output; and the profile library plus integration with partner tube-design software (SolidWorks add-in via the SPI/PDM connection).

Primary evidence modules: tc_rohr/ (Rohr = tube) with sub-modules roh_to3d (2D↔3D / unrolling), roh_tech/rohr (geometry technology), roh_tech/maschine (machine kinematics & supports), roh_tech/roh_teo (TEO exchange files), roh_tech/roh_rpf (RPF profile files), roh_tech/roh_alp (ALP/APD output), roh_asm (interactive command automats), roh_prf/roh_prf_lib (profile geometry & library), roh_grit (GRIT dialogs), and tc_interface/tc_rohr (public API). The Tube and Tube_inch database schemas hold the persistent data.

Editions served: ToPs Tube (tube option), ToPs 400 (underlying laser platform). See [[01 - Vision & Scope]] and [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]].

2. Capability Overview

  • Read a tube part's 3D geometry (.rot/.rmt files) and triangulate it into a 3D view; maintain a solid 3D workpiece model (cylinder/rectangle bodies, cutting bodies, penetration contours).
  • Support the full family of cross-sections: round tube, rectangular tube, flat-oval, custom/special closed profile, open L/U/C profiles, open special profiles and flat material.
  • Unroll (Abwicklung) the tube skin to a 2D drawing and wrap 2D contours back onto the 3D tube; toggle representation and machining visibility.
  • Maintain a profile library (create/read/modify/delete) parameterised by cross-section, wall thickness, corner radius, height, width, diameter, material, backed by RPF geometry files and the PROFILTAB/TUBETECH tables.
  • Assign machining cases to the tube: planar cut, perpendicular cut, tube-end/miter cut, insert, mount and through-penetration, with corner treatment, lead-in/lead-out and weld-seam positioning.
  • Look up and apply tube laser technology and NC cycles from the technology rules and cycle tables, keyed on cross-section/material/dimension.
  • Configure and place tube handling elements: chuck, tube guides, bridges and finished-part supports with operating modes.
  • Check machining/tube/machine collisions; optimize machining sequence along the tube (sectors, direction, rotary axis, chuck-area avoidance).
  • Define measuring cycles for in-machine tube measurement.
  • Generate ToPs 400 NC/APD output containing tube geometry and technology.
  • Integrate with partner tube-CAD (SolidWorks add-in) via TEO exchange files, the Tube database (ODBC, metric & inch) and TruTops PDM, protected by a dongle/licence.

4. Functional Requirements

FR-TUBE-001 — Import and triangulate tube 3D geometry

  • Requirement: When a tube part 3D geometry file (.rot/.rmt stream) is opened, the system shall parse it and decompose it into a triangle-and-line geometry for 3D display.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: The 3D tube must be visualised before any machining can be programmed.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_to3d/RotTo3d/TcRotTo3d.{h,cpp} ("liest eine rot-datei … zerlegt es in Dreiecke und Linien"); TiGeometrySink; roh_to3d/RotTo3d/Converter/TcKonverterObjekt.h (".rot/.rmt-Datei").
  • Verification: Test (load a .rot/.rmt and confirm rendered triangulated 3D body).

FR-TUBE-002 — Solid 3D tube workpiece model

  • Requirement: The system shall represent the tube as a solid workpiece model composed of sub-bodies (cylinder and rectangle bodies), cutting bodies and their penetration contours.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Cuts are computed as intersections of cutting bodies with the tube; a solid model is the basis for all machining and collision logic.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_to3d/RotTo3d/ToPs400/ classes TcT4Werkstueck, TcT4Zylinder, TcT4Rechteck, TcT4Teilkoerper, TcT4Schnittkoerper, TcT4Durchdringung; T4_WERKSTUECK/T4_ZYLINDER/T4_RECHTECK/T4_SCHNITTKOERPER/T4_DURCHDRINGUNG in tc_interface/tc_rohr/rohrDaten.h.
  • Verification: Analysis (inspect model object graph for a machined tube).

FR-TUBE-003 — Unroll tube surface and wrap contours

  • Requirement: The system shall unroll (Abwicklung) the tube surface into a 2D development and shall wrap 2D contours back onto the 3D tube surface.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Programming on the flat development is the core tube-CAM workflow; the wrap-back keeps 2D edits synchronised with the 3D model.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_tech/rohr/abwick.cpp, aufwick.cpp (DD_Kontur::wickle_dich_auf, schliesse_abwicklung_kontur); tc_interface/tc_rohr/rohrFunc.h (rohWickleWerkstueckAuf, rohOrgKonturWickelAuf).
  • Verification: Test (compare an edge length/position on 3D model versus its unrolled counterpart).

FR-TUBE-004 — Representation and machining-visibility state

  • Requirement: While a tube part is open, the system shall allow the operator to switch between the unrolled (Abwicklung) and 3D (Raum) representations, each shown with or without machining.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Operators verify geometry in 3D and program details on the development; toggling machining visibility aids inspection.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_tech/rohr/enum.h E_rohr_state {abwick_ohne_bearb, abwick_mit_bearb, raum_ohne_bearb, raum_mit_bearb}; GRIT masks M_ROHR_MENU_3D_MESSEN, DARSTELLUNG/REPRESENTATION in roh_grit/roh_grit.a26.
  • Verification: Demonstration (toggle each of the four states).

FR-TUBE-005 — Supported cross-section (profile) families

  • Requirement: The system shall support the tube cross-section types round tube, rectangular tube, flat-oval, custom/special closed profile, open L/U/C profiles, open special profile and flat material.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Covers the geometry families a tube laser must program.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_prf_lib/TiProfile.h TeProfileTyp {e_Rundrohr, e_Rechteck, e_Sonderprofil, e_Flachoval, e_LProfil, e_UProfil, e_CProfil, e_Flachmaterial, e_OffenesSonderprofil}; rohrDaten.h (RUND, EBENE, R_RADIUS, BELIEBIGES_PROFIL, FLACHMATERIAL, BELIEBIGES_OFFENES_PROFIL, FLACHOVAL); roh_tech/rohr/enum.h TeOffenesProfil {e_LProfil, e_UProfil, e_CProfil}.
  • Verification: Test (create/program one part per cross-section type).

FR-TUBE-006 — Round-tube basic data

  • Requirement: When the operator selects a round tube, the system shall accept and validate its basic data (material, wall thickness, diameter).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Round tube is the primary cross-section; its parameters drive geometry and technology lookup.
  • Source: GRIT masks M_ROHR_GRUND_DATEN_RUND, M_ROHR_MAT_RUND, fields ST_ROHR_ProfilErstellenDurchmesser/…Blechdicke/…Material and F_ERSTELLE_PROFIL_AUS_RMT_RUND in roh_grit/roh_grit.a26.
  • Verification: Test (enter round-tube data; confirm acceptance and downstream geometry).

FR-TUBE-007 — Rectangular-tube basic data with corner radius

  • Requirement: When the operator selects a rectangular tube, the system shall accept and validate its basic data (material, wall thickness, width, height, corner radius).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Rectangular sections need width/height plus corner radius to model the rounded corners correctly.
  • Source: GRIT masks M_ROHR_GRUND_DATEN_ECKIG, M_ROHR_MAT_ECKIG, fields ST_ROHR_ProfilErstellenBreite/…Hoehe/…Eckenradius, F_ERSTELLE_PROFIL_AUS_RMT_RECH; TUBETECH columns TT_BREITE,TT_HOEHE,TT_ECKENRADIUS.
  • Verification: Test (enter rectangular data incl. corner radius).

FR-TUBE-008 — Open-profile (L/U/C) parametrisation

  • Requirement: Where an open profile (L, U or C) is selected, the system shall accept its detailed parameters, including per-wall thicknesses, leg lengths (Schenkel) and inner/outer edge radii.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Open profiles require richer parameters than closed tubes; this is an edition/profile-dependent capability.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_prf_lib/TiProfile.h (setParameterLProfile, setParameterUProfile, setParameterCProfile, getOPWandstaerke2..5, getOPSchenkel1/2, getOPKantenradiusAussen/Innen*); TUBETECH (TT_SCHENKELLAENGE*, TT_WANDSTAERKE_2..5, TT_INNENRADIUS*).
  • Verification: Test (create an L/U/C profile and verify stored parameters).

FR-TUBE-009 — Profile library management

  • Requirement: The system shall allow the operator to create, read, modify and delete tube profiles held in the profile library, each identified by a unique name and classified by cross-section type.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Profiles are reusable master data shared across tube parts and jobs.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_prf_lib/TiProfileLibrary.h (getAllProfiles, getProfileById, createProfile, saveProfile, deleteProfile); TiProfile.h (getUniqueName, getQuerschnitt); GRIT mask file roh_grit/prf_verw.a26.
  • Verification: Test (round-trip create → edit → delete a profile).

FR-TUBE-010 — RPF profile geometry file with open/closed validation

  • Requirement: The system shall read and write profile cross-section geometry as RPF files and shall report an error if the profile is invalidly open when a closed profile is required (or vice versa).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: The RPF file is the persisted cross-section geometry; open/closed validity is a precondition for wrapping and cutting.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_tech/roh_rpf/ (TcTubeRpfFacade, TeTubeRpfEnums.h TeTubeRpfError {…e_tubeRpfProfileClosed, e_tubeRpfProfileOpen, e_tubeRpfFileError}); TiProfile::getRPFFileName.
  • Verification: Test (load valid/invalid RPFs; confirm error codes).

FR-TUBE-011 — Match/create tube technology record from imported RMT

  • Requirement: When a raw-material tube (RMT) is loaded whose profile ID is unknown, the system shall search the technology table for a matching record by cross-section, material and dimensions and, if none matches, shall offer to create a new profile from the RMT parameters.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Imported tube stock must be bound to a technology/profile definition before machining.
  • Source: GRIT MMK/mini_sql in roh_grit/roh_grit.a26 — SELECT :TT_RECORD_NAME FROM TUBETECH WHERE TT_QUERSCHNITT=… AND TT_MATERIAL LIKE … AND TT_WANDSTAERKE=… AND TT_DURCHMESSER=… (round) / … TT_BREITE/TT_HOEHE/TT_ECKENRADIUS (rectangular); SELECT :MAT_ROHR_IDNR FROM MAT_ROHR; masks F_ERSTELLE_PROFIL_AUS_RMT_*.
  • Verification: Test (import RMT with/without matching TUBETECH record).

FR-TUBE-012 — Planar and perpendicular cut cases

  • Requirement: The system shall let the operator assign planar-cut (Ebener Schnitt) and perpendicular-cut (Senkrechtschnitt) machining cases to the tube.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: These are the fundamental cut types for tube ends and openings.
  • Source: rohrDaten.h (EBENER_SCHNITT 13, SENKRECHT_SCHNITT 15); roh_asm/roh_faelle.{hpp,cpp}; masks M_ROHR_MENU_HAUPT_BEARB, SCHNITT.
  • Verification: Test (create both case types on a tube).

FR-TUBE-013 — Insert, mount and through-penetration cases

  • Requirement: The system shall let the operator assign insert (Einsatz), mount (Aufsetzen) and through-penetration (Durchstecken) machining cases between intersecting tubes/bodies.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Tube-to-tube joints (T-joints, saddles, through holes) are core tube-fabrication features.
  • Source: rohrDaten.h (EINSATZ 10, AUFSETZEN 11, DURCHSTECKEN 12); masks M_ROHR_EINSTECHEN, M_ROHR_DURCHSTECKEN, M_ROHREINSATZ_*, M_ROHRAUFSATZ_*; roh_tech/rohr/einsatz.cpp, durch.{h,cpp}.
  • Verification: Test (create insert/mount/through cases and inspect resulting penetration contours).

FR-TUBE-014 — Tube-end / miter cuts

  • Requirement: The system shall let the operator define tube-end cuts on the left and right ends of round and rectangular tubes, including an end cut angle (miter).
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: End/miter cuts are needed to fit tubes together and to trim stock to length.
  • Source: masks M_ROHRENDE_LINKS, M_ROHRENDE_RECHTS, M_ROHRENDE_LINKS_ECKIG, M_ROHRENDE_RECHTS_ECKIG, M_ROHR_WINKEL; rohrDaten.h RECHTS/LINKS.
  • Verification: Test (define left/right end cuts at an angle).

FR-TUBE-015 — Reject inadmissible machining parameters

  • Requirement: If a machining parameter is out of the admissible range — for example cut angle, twist angle, cut position, length, diameter, wall thickness, offset or roughness depth — then the system shall reject the operation and report the corresponding error.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Guards against non-manufacturable geometry and machine faults.
  • Source: tc_interface/tc_rohr/rohrDaten.h error defines RO_UNZULAESSIGER_SCHNITTWINKEL, RO_UNZULAESSIGER_VERDREHWINKEL, RO_UNZULAESSIGE_SCHNITT_POSITION, RO_UNZULAESSIGE_LAENGE, RO_UNZULAESSIGER_DURCHMESSER, RO_UNZULAESSIGE_BLECHDICKE, RO_UNZULAESSIGER_VERSATZ_OD_R2, RO_UNZULAESSIGE_RAUHTIEFE.
  • Verification: Test (supply each out-of-range value; confirm rejection).

FR-TUBE-016 — Enforce maximum laser cut angle

  • Requirement: If a requested cut would require a laser incidence angle greater than 45°, then the system shall reject the cut and report the excess-angle condition.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: The tube laser head cannot maintain cut quality beyond its tilt limit.
  • Source: rohrDaten.h RO_LASERWINKEL_GROESSER_45 33064, RO_EBENE_BEARBEITUNG_UNMOEGLICH 33063.
  • Verification: Test (define a cut exceeding 45°; confirm rejection).

FR-TUBE-017 — Corner treatment strategy

  • Requirement: The system shall apply a configurable corner-treatment strategy to profile corners, including corner/start/end radii, lead-in (Vorlauf) and lead-out (Nachlauf), LTT points/zones and edge-influence points.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Correct corner handling on rectangular/open profiles is required for edge quality and machine reachability, and is re-initialised per machine.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_asm/roh_cornerstrategy.{h,cpp} (init_roh_corner_strategy_ASM_states); rohrDaten.h corner flags ECKE, START_RADIUS, ENDE_RADIUS, START_LTT, ENDE_LTT, LTT_PKT, VORLAUF_START, NACHLAUF_ENDE, MITTE_KANTE, KANTENEINFLUSS_PKT; masks ECKENBEHANDLUNG, RUNDUNG.
  • Verification: Inspection (verify corner geometry and lead moves against strategy settings).

FR-TUBE-018 — Weld-seam position handling

  • Requirement: For a closed welded profile, the system shall determine the weld-seam position and reorder the contour so that machining is referenced to the seam element.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: The longitudinal weld of a welded tube must be positioned/avoided consistently; contour ordering fixes the seam reference.
  • Source: tc_rohr/roh_prf/nahtlage.{h,cpp} (pruefe_elemlist_auf_nahtlage, db_aendere_kontur_startelement); masks NAHT* references.
  • Verification: Analysis (verify seam element becomes the contour start on a closed profile).

FR-TUBE-019 — Tube laser technology lookup

  • Requirement: When machining is generated, the system shall retrieve the applicable tube laser cutting technology (feed/power/gas parameters) from the tube technology tables keyed on cross-section, material and dimensions.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Cut quality depends on material/section-specific laser parameters.
  • Source: Tube schema tables LASERTAB, LASERTECHNO, LASERTECHNO2, LASERTECHNOBC, LASERTOPS, TUBETECH (TT_LTT_DATENSATZ, TT_MATERIAL, TT_QUERSCHNITT); MAP_LTT_FOOTNOTES; roh_tech/rohr technology handling.
  • Verification: Test (verify parameters resolved for a given material/section).

FR-TUBE-020 — Technology-rule resolution

  • Requirement: The system shall resolve machining/technology rules from the tube rule tables according to the tube's material, cross-section and dimensions.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Rule tables encode the manufacturer/machine-specific defaults applied to each feature.
  • Source: Tube schema REGEL_ALLG, REGEL10, REGEL11, REGEL12, REGEL20, REGEL101, REGEL111, REGEL121, REGEL201, MAS_REGEL, REGEL10_ALIAS; roh_asm "Regelwerk"/initRegelwerk (TcFertigteilunterstuetzung::initRegelwerk).
  • Verification: Analysis (trace rule selection for a sample part).

FR-TUBE-021 — NC-cycle assignment

  • Requirement: The system shall assign machine/control-specific NC cycles to tube machining and shall allow customer-specific cycle definitions.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Tube laser controls require named NC cycles per machine/control version; some are customer copies.
  • Source: Tube schema NCZYKLEN (NC_ZYKLUS, NC_MANAME, NC_STEUERUNG, NC_ST_VERSION, NC_KUNDEN_COPY, NC_AKTIVE), NCZYKLUS_STV, ZYKLUS_NAME, NC_KUNDE, NC_KOPFTAB_MASCHINE; mask M_ROHR_NC_BEARBEITUNG, M_ROHR_MENU_NC.
  • Verification: Test (assign cycles and confirm they appear in NC output).

FR-TUBE-022 — Tube machine selection and kinematic limits

  • Requirement: When a tube machine is selected, the system shall load its kinematic working-area and travel limits (per axis) and shall constrain machining to those limits.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Machining must stay within the specific tube laser's reachable envelope.
  • Source: Tube schema MASCHINE, MAS_ROHR (MA_ROHR_ARBEITB_A/B/C_P/M, MA_ROHR_UEBERFA_*, MA_ROHR_GESMAX/GESMIN, MA_ROHR_ROTOLAS), MAS_ALLG, MAS_GRUPPE; roh_tech/maschine/TcRohrschneideMaschine.{h,cpp}, TcTubeMachineConfigurationSync; mask M_ROHR_MASCHINE_VORWAHL.
  • Verification: Inspection (verify limits loaded and enforced).

FR-TUBE-023 — Tube guiding elements (chuck, guides, bridges)

  • Requirement: The system shall model the tube-guiding elements — chuck (Rohrfutter), tube guides (Rohrführung) and bridges (Brücke) — using their machine-configured geometry (envelope-circle diameter, form shells, rollers).
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Correct guiding geometry is needed for collision checking and support positioning on guided (Tubematic) machines.
  • Source: roh_tech/maschine/TcRohrfuehrung.{h,cpp}, TcBruecke.{h,cpp} ("um das Rohr bei der Bearbeitung zu führen (Tubematic)"); Tube schema ROHRFUEHRUNGEN (RFG_HUELLKREISDURCHMESSER, RFG_FORMSCHALE_*), ROHRFUTTER, ROHRFUTTER_MAS.
  • Verification: Inspection (verify guide/chuck geometry from configuration).

FR-TUBE-024 — Finished-part support (FUS) with operating modes

  • Requirement: Where finished-part support is available, the system shall place and control supports according to a selected operating mode (none, preset, with lift/lower, moving-along) subject to their positions and maximum load.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Prevents the cut-off finished section from dropping or colliding on machines equipped with FUS.
  • Source: roh_tech/rohr/enum.h TeFUS_Betriebsarten {e_Ohne, e_Voreingestellt, e_MitHebenSenken, e_Mitfahrend}; roh_tech/maschine/TcFertigteilunterstuetzung.{h,cpp}; Tube schema MAS_FUS_VALUES (MA_FUS_X_AUSDEHNUNG, MA_FUS_MAXBELASTUNG, MA_FUS_PARKPOS, MA_FUS_SPERRBEREICH, MA_FUS_ENABLED), MAS_FUS_LIMIT_VALUES.
  • Verification: Test (select each FUS mode; verify support placement and load check).

FR-TUBE-025 — Support placement (Auflager)

  • Requirement: The system shall let the operator set, move and delete tube supports (Auflager) along the tube, respecting configured support count, spacing and dead zones.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Supports keep long stock stable; their placement must respect the machine's support layout.
  • Source: roh_asm/roh_aufl.{cpp,hpp}, roh_tech/maschine/TcAuflager.{h,cpp}; masks O_AUFLAGERSETZEN/O_AUFLAGERLOESCHEN/O_AUFLAGERVERSCHIEBEN, AUFLAGER; Tube schema MAS_ROHRUNTERSTUETZUNGEN (MA_ROHRUS_ANZAHL, MA_ROHRUS_PLAETZE, MA_ROHRUS_X_INK, MA_ROHRUS_TOTBE, MA_ROHRUS_FIXPOS_FUS1..8), T4_AUFLAGER.
  • Verification: Test (place/move/delete supports; confirm constraints).

FR-TUBE-026 — Collision checking

  • Requirement: The system shall check tube machining for collisions between the machining/head path and the tube or machine elements and shall report detected collisions.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: Collision-free programs are required before NC output on a tube machine.
  • Source: tc_interface/tc_rohr/rohrFunc.h (pruefeRohrKollision(S_koll_pool*, S_kontur*)); masks KOLLISIONEN/COLLISIONS.
  • Verification: Test (introduce a colliding case; confirm it is flagged).

FR-TUBE-027 — Machining-sequence optimization along the tube

  • Requirement: The system shall optimize the machining sequence over the unrolled tube using a configurable optimizer that controls sectors per unrolling, sector width, machining and rotary-axis direction, arc/flat strategies and chuck-area avoidance.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Sequence optimization reduces cycle time and avoids machining in the clamped chuck zone.
  • Source: Tube schema ROHROPTIMIERER (ROPT_SECTORSPERUNROLLING, ROPT_SECTORWIDTH, ROPT_MACHININGDIRECTION, ROPT_ROTARYAXISDIRECTION, ROPT_STRATEGY_ARC/FLAT, ROPT_RECT_CHUCKAREA, ROPT_RECT_USECHUCKAREA); roh_asm/roh_iopti.{cpp,hpp}; masks WEGOPTIMIEREN/M_ROHR_WEGOPTI_DSF, OPTIMIS.
  • Verification: Test (vary optimizer settings; verify resulting sequence).

FR-TUBE-028 — In-machine measuring cycles

  • Requirement: Where measuring is supported, the system shall let the operator define tube measuring cycles (measuring variant/type, centre reference) for in-machine measurement.
  • Priority: Optional
  • Rationale: Measuring cycles compensate for stock deviation before/during cutting.
  • Source: roh_asm/roh_mes.{cpp,hpp} (messVariante, messArt, center_flag); masks M_ROHR_MENU_3D_MESSEN, M_ROHR_MESS_ZYKLEN_RUND, M_ROHR_MESS_ZYKLEN_ECKIG, M_ROHR_MESS_PARAM.
  • Verification: Test (define a measuring cycle and confirm it is stored/output).

FR-TUBE-029 — Part unloading / evacuation

  • Requirement: Where automatic unloading is available, the system shall let the operator assign unloading (Entsorgen/Ausschleusen) to cut parts, including unload channel, part-quittance and evacuation movements.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Automated tube lines must evacuate finished parts and offcuts and book them; this is machine/edition dependent.
  • Source: roh_asm/roh_ents.{cpp,hpp}; mask fields O_entsEntladekanal*, O_entsTeilequittung*, O_entsAusschleusBewegung, O_entsVerbuchungspflicht, O_ENTSSETZEN/O_ENTSLOESCHEN; masks AUSSCHLEUSEN, ENTLADEN/DECHARGEMENT.
  • Verification: Test (assign unloading and inspect generated movements).

FR-TUBE-030 — ToPs 400 NC / APD output

  • Requirement: When NC output is requested for a tube part, the system shall write a ToPs 400 output file (APD/ALP) containing the tube geometry and the resolved technology.
  • Priority: Essential
  • Rationale: The output file is the deliverable consumed by NC post-processing for the tube laser.
  • Source: tc_interface/tc_rohr/rohrApWrite.h (t4_write_400, t4_write_rohr, t4_write_typ_info, t4_write_400_part); roh_tech/roh_alp/ (ro_apwri.cpp, ro_apcal.cpp, rohrAlpinDaten.h "Techno ToPs400"); roh_prf_main/ToPs400Export.exe.
  • Verification: Test (generate output and verify geometry + technology blocks).

FR-TUBE-031 — Partner tube-CAD / PDM integration

  • Requirement: Where the tube-design partner integration is licensed, the system shall connect partner tube-CAD (SolidWorks add-in) to TruTops Tube resources — the metric/inch tube database (ODBC DSN), the profile file path and TruTops PDM — subject to a valid dongle/licence.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: Tube parts are frequently designed in SolidWorks and handed to TruTops Tube via a licensed connection.
  • Source: tc_rohr/TubeConnectionDll/publicHeader/TiTubeConnection.h (initialize, getTubeDSN(e_metric/e_usCustomary), getTubeProfilePath, isLicenseValid, openBrowser); tc_rohr/TubeSwAddin/ (SolidWorks add-in).
  • Verification: Demonstration (connect from add-in with/without valid licence).

FR-TUBE-032 — TEO tube-exchange file handling

  • Requirement: The system shall create, read and save TEO tube-exchange files containing tube contours organised into groups, using the PDM browser for storage.
  • Priority: Conditional
  • Rationale: TEO is the contour/technology exchange format between partner tube-CAD and TruTops Tube.
  • Source: roh_tech/roh_teo/ (TcTubeTeoManager, TcTubeTeoCreator, TcTubeTeoExtractor, TcTubeTeoGroupIterator, TiTubeTeoContourIterator — "Konturen einer Teo-Datei", "Konturgruppen"); TiTubeConnection::openSaveTeoBrowser.
  • Verification: Test (round-trip a TEO with grouped contours).

5. External Interfaces (area-specific)

Detailed interface requirements live in [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]; this area touches:

  • File formats: .rot/.rmt (tube 3D geometry / raw-material tube), .rpf (profile cross-section geometry), .teo (tube exchange object with grouped contours), ALP/APD (ToPs 400 NC output). See FR-TUBE-001, -010, -030, -032.
  • Databases: Tube and Tube_inch SQL schemas (profiles PROFILTAB/TUBETECH, materials MAT_ROHR, machines MASCHINE/MAS_ROHR, supports MAS_ROHRUNTERSTUETZUNGEN/MAS_FUS_VALUES, guides ROHRFUEHRUNGEN/ROHRFUTTER, optimizer ROHROPTIMIERER, laser tech LASERTECHNO*, rules REGEL*, cycles NCZYKLEN), accessed via ODBC DSN (metric & inch) resolved through TiTubeConnection::getTubeDSN.
  • PDM / partner CAD: TruTops PDM connection over CORBA and the SolidWorks tube add-in (TubeSwAddin, TubeConnectionDll). See FR-TUBE-031/-032.
  • Licensing: dongle/password check via the tube connection DLL. See FR-TUBE-031 and [[40 - Non-Functional Requirements]].
  • Machines: TRUMPF tube laser controls; ToPs 400 / ToPs 800 painter variants exist in roh_to3d. See [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]] and [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]].

6. Assumptions & Constraints

Cross-cutting items are catalogued in [[50 - Constraints & Assumptions]]. Area-specific:

  • The tube geometry model, terminology and persisted files are German-language legacy (Rohr, Abwicklung, Durchdringung, Auflager, Fertigteilunterstützung); see [[04 - Domain Model & Glossary]].
  • Tube machining is built on the ToPs 400 laser platform; the tube option (ToPs Tube) is a licensed add-on — several requirements are marked Conditional accordingly.
  • Both metric and inch databases exist in parallel (Tube / Tube_inch); the active DSN is selected by measurement system.
  • The area assumes an available cutting-body / penetration solid modeller (roh_to3d T4 classes) and the shared CAD geometry kernel (contours, arcs, lines) documented in [[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]].
  • Laser incidence is limited to ≤ 45° (FR-TUBE-016); geometry that cannot be produced within machine kinematics is rejected rather than approximated.
  • tc_profil (the TcProfil* classes: bumping/folding/bending calculators) appears to be shared sheet-metal profile/bending geometry used by CAD, not the tube profile library — see Open Questions.

7. Open Questions & Ambiguities

  • Bar-stock nesting. The assignment scope mentions "tube nesting on bar stock", but the strongest evidence found (ROHROPTIMIERER, MAT_ROHR.MAT_ROHR_ABMESS_L) concerns machining-sequence optimization and single-part stock dimensions. Whether multi-part length nesting / offcut (remnant) management on a bar is performed here or in a separate tube-planning product could not be confirmed from tc_rohr. (inferred)
  • tc_profil ownership. The tc_profil module (TcProfilBiegung, TcProfilFalzung, TcProfilBumpingBerechner, …) reads as sheet-metal bending/folding profile geometry shared with CAD, not tube cross-sections. It likely belongs to [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]] or [[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]]; flagged for the owning analyst.
  • aTubeFunc / aTulasFunc / aTucadFunc. These names appear only as OLB link targets in tc_bof/olbmakefile; the corresponding GRIT source (.a26) for the Tube/Tulas/Tucad application function tables was not located in the snapshot, so the exact command inventory beyond roh_grit.a26/prf_verw.a26 is incomplete.
  • klietsch.cpp algorithm. roh_tech/rohr/klietsch.{cpp,h} (with a debug variant) is clearly a named geometric algorithm in the penetration/unrolling path, but its precise function (developer eponym) could not be determined.
  • roh_teo perpendicular calculators. TcTubeTeoOpenPerpCalculator / TcTubeTeoClosedPerpCalculator compute per-contour perpendiculars for open vs. closed profiles; the exact downstream use (bevel angle vs. approach normal) was not fully traced.
  • ToPs 800 painter. roh_to3d/ToPs800Painter suggests support for a newer machine generation than ToPs 400; the functional difference in machining capability (vs. the ToPs 400 path) is not documented in headers.
  • Roughness-depth (Rauhtiefe) machining. Error codes and masks reference roughness-depth checks (RO_UNZULAESSIGE_RAUHTIEFE, GRUND_RAUHTIEFE); whether this drives a distinct surface-finish machining mode or only validation is unclear.
