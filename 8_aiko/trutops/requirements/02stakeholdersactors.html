02StakeholdersActors

product: trutopscad org: trumpf title: "Stakeholders & Actors" doc: "02 - Stakeholders & Actors" id-prefix: "-" srs-standard: "ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018" status: reverse-engineered-draft source: "TruTopsCAD legacy source snapshot (files dated May 2024)" related: ["[[00 - Index & Reading Guide]]", "[[01 - Vision & Scope]]", "[[03 - System Context & Architecture Overview]]", "[[04 - Domain Model & Glossary]]", "[[30 - External Interface Requirements]]", "[[40 - Non-Functional Requirements]]"] tags: [requirements, trutops, stakeholders]

Stakeholders & Actors

Reverse-engineered requirement draft — derived from static analysis of legacy source, NOT an authoritative specification. Actors and stakeholders are reconstructed from the roles that the code, GRIT dialogs, licensing checks and database access paths imply; they are not drawn from an original stakeholder register.

0. Purpose & Method

This document identifies the human actors, external system actors and stakeholders of the TruTops CAD/CAM suite and links each to the functional areas they exercise. It is the shared cast list for the whole SRS set: the per-area functional documents ([[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]] … [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]]) name actors locally; this document consolidates them, removes duplication, and gives each a role, goals and a map to its primary FR documents.

Definitions of the German domain terms used below (Werkstoff, Tafel, Auftrag, Regelwerk, Mikrosteg, Abwicklung, …) live in [[04 - Domain Model & Glossary]]. The concrete protocols, file formats and device contracts behind the external actors are specified in [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]. The quality attributes that each stakeholder cares about are specified in [[40 - Non-Functional Requirements]].

"Actor" is used in the 29148 / use-case sense: a role a person plays, or an external system that exchanges data with TruTops across a system boundary. One physical person routinely plays several actor roles (a small shop's owner may be CAD designer, CAM programmer, nesting operator and machine operator at once); the roles are separated here because their goals and the FR areas they touch differ.

TruTops is delivered as a family of licensed editions (ToPsCad, ToPs 300 punch, ToPs 600 bend, ToPs 100 laser, ToPs Tube, ToPsNest, ToPs 600 bend data, plus Lite/2000 variants — see [[01 - Vision & Scope]]). Which actor roles are available to a given site depends on the licensed editions, enforced by the dongle/Wibu + per-format password licensing subsystem (licenseCheck, TcLicenseCheck) with a service-only superuser bypass (tops_set_superuser / superuser_of_type).

1. Human Actors

Grouped by category. The Primary FR docs column lists the functional documents each role principally drives; most roles also touch the shared UI ([[22 - FR - UI Framework, Dialogs & Interaction (GRIT)]]) and visualization ([[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]]) layers, called out only where central.

1.1 Primary production users

Actor Role & Goals Primary FR docs
CAD Designer (Konstrukteur) Creates and edits 2D sheet-metal part geometry: primitives, parametric hole macros, transformations, fillets/chamfers/notches, dimensioning, parametric variants. Goal: produce clean, manufacturable part drawings and reusable geometry. Chiefly a ToPsCad / ToPs 400 Cad user. [[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]], [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]], [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]], [[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]]
CAM / NC Programmer (punch · laser · combi · tube) Turns geometry into a machine-ready program: assigns cutting/punch technology and rulesets, generates lead-ins/piercing/micro-joints, applies tooling, sequences and optimises motion, and emits controller-specific NC. Goal: a correct, collision-free, cycle-time-optimised NC program on the target machine. Editions ToPs 300 / ToPs 100 / ToPs Tube. [[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]], [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]], [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]], [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]], [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]]
Bending Programmer / Press-brake Operator Defines bends (kind, method, technique, angle, radius), computes bend-deduction factors and flat patterns, assigns punch/die tools and builds bent profiles. Goal: a foldable part with a correct Abwicklung and valid tool set. Edition ToPs 600 (data from ToPs 600). [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]], [[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]], [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]]
Nesting Operator / Work Planner (Arbeitsvorbereiter) Arranges order parts onto raw/remnant sheets (true-shape, rectangular, tile), tunes utilization, manages remnants and stock deduction, and palletizes good parts/scrap/skeleton for automation. Goal: maximum material yield and a producible sheet layout. Stand-alone ToPsNest and embedded in ToPs 300/100. [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]], [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[20 - FR - Production Planning & Projects]]
Production Planner Groups captured parts into nesting orders (quantities, priority, due date), builds programming projects (PPJ) that bind order → machine → material → sheets → parts, assembles production plans and hands jobs to the shop floor / MES. Goal: on-time, machine-loaded production. [[20 - FR - Production Planning & Projects]], [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]], [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]]
Machine Operator (shop-floor / touch-panel) Runs the programming project / auto-run per sheet, views parts and nests, prints setup plans, and initiates NC transfer at the machine. Goal: correct, safe execution of the program with minimal manual intervention. [[20 - FR - Production Planning & Projects]], [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]], [[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]]

1.2 Administrators & data owners

Actor Role & Goals Primary FR docs
Technology & Rules Administrator Maintains machines (identity, work area, axis dynamics, peripherals, control-version compatibility), rulesets (Regelwerk), materials, laser technology tables (LTT), AutoCut and micro-joint configurations, and processing patterns. Goal: master data that yields correct automatic technology assignment across all machines. [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]], [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]], [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]]
Tool Administrator (incl. central ITM/SW06 tool manager) Maintains punch tools, dies, multi-tools, thread formers, cassettes and named tool lists; imports/exports tool XML; connects to networked/central tool management (ITM via the SW06 wrapper) for live tool availability. Goal: accurate, available tooling for programming. [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[13 - FR - Punching (ToPs 300)]]
Bend Tool / Bend Database Administrator Creates and edits standard bend-tool geometry via the bend tool assistant (Biegewerkzeugassistent) and maintains the ToPs 600 bend database (bend-deduction factors, punch/die/hemming/Z/swivel tool tables, crowning). Goal: valid press-brake tooling and bend-factor tables. [[16 - FR - Bending (ToPs 600)]], [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]]
System / Database Administrator Deploys the SQL Server / attached-MDF databases and ODBC DSNs, applies DATADICT-driven schema creation, versioning and migration (incl. metric↔inch porting), and manages access roles and licensing. Goal: consistent, versioned, accessible data stores. [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]], [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]
PDM User Browses and checks documents in the TRUMPF.NET / TRUMPF PDM vault through the transparent OmAdapter file access and the PDM browser. Goal: managed, versioned access to parts and drawings. [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]], [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]]

1.3 Technical, integration & support actors

Actor Role & Goals Primary FR docs
Automation / Integration Engineer Writes Python 2.5 scripts (parametric variants, menu buttons, TruTops_customize.py), drives the ASM/MMK interpreters, and builds .NET clients against the per-edition Xidl component API, the Fab adapter and the headless reproduction / laser shop-floor-programming DLLs. Goal: batch/headless and MES-integrated operation. [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]], [[20 - FR - Production Planning & Projects]], [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]]
UI / Mask & Customization Developer Authors .gritx masks and MMK/mini_sql scripts, defines dialogs, finder lists and validation, and debugs with the MMK step debugger. Goal: correct, localisable interactive dialogs bound to the right data. [[22 - FR - UI Framework, Dialogs & Interaction (GRIT)]], [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]], [[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]]
System Integrator Embeds the ActiveX/OCX GeoViewer in host applications, and configures colour/font/icon tables, plotter profiles, units, language and the mask set via .org/.ini files. Goal: TruTops (or its viewer) fitted into a wider tool/portal landscape. [[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]], [[22 - FR - UI Framework, Dialogs & Interaction (GRIT)]], [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]
Partner Tube-CAD Designer Designs tubes/profiles in SolidWorks via the TRUMPF tube add-in and exchanges TEO tube-contour objects with TruTops Tube through PDM. Goal: a smooth partner-CAD → tube-CAM handoff. [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]
QA / Regression Engineer Records interactive command traces (TraceMode / .taf) and replays them through the headless reproduction pipeline for regression. Goal: repeatable verification of nesting/programming behaviour. [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]], [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]]
TRUMPF Service & Support Staff Runs data porting/migration, inspects logs and table dumps, and uses the superuser mode to bypass edition/licence gating during support. Goal: diagnose and repair a customer installation. [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]], [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]]

Note on installers / upgrade processes. The installer/upgrade job that applies schema and data migrations is an automated process rather than a person, but it acts with administrator authority; its behaviour is specified as part of DB versioning & migration in [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]].

2. External System Actors

Systems and devices that exchange data with TruTops across a system boundary. The Interface doc ref column points to where the contract is specified — the consolidated [[30 - External Interface Requirements]] and/or the owning FR document.

2.1 Machines, controllers & shop-floor automation

System Interaction Interface doc ref
CNC machine controllers (punch presses, combi punch-laser, laser cutters, tube lasers, press brakes) Receive controller-specific NC programs and cycles generated from the finished layout; keyed by machine + control + control-version. Multiple controller families observed (e.g. Siemens SIN840D, KemroK2, press-brake DA24+58). [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]], [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]]
Loading / unloading & sorting automation (LiftMaster, SortMaster, TrumaGrip, lift frames with suction/gripper devices) Consume sheet-load strategies, unloading operations, holding-device assignments, disposal zones and pallet/sorting-compartment definitions; drive automatic part removal and palletizing. [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]], [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]], [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]]
NcLink / DNC transfer service Sequences NC programs, formats the production plan (.LST / .TPP plus HTML/TXT/XML overview) and transfers programs to the machine control. [[20 - FR - Production Planning & Projects]], [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]]
External machine-simulation software Consumes a compressed machine-simulation data archive exported from unloading/lift/kinematics data. [[18 - FR - NC Generation & Motion Optimization]], [[14 - FR - Laser Cutting (ToPs 100)]]

2.2 Enterprise, data-management & tool-management systems

System Interaction Interface doc ref
TruTops Fab (MES / PPS) Supplies production orders (part, material, quantity, due date, machine, rotatability) via a registry-discovered, licence-checked PPS plugin; receives nesting-job validation and job/file lifecycle feedback (notify / declare-done). [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]], [[20 - FR - Production Planning & Projects]]
TRUMPF PDM / TRUMPF.NET vault File access, browsing, and attribute/catalog queries routed through the OmAdapter / TiPdmFacet facade over CORBA; also the tube TEO exchange channel. [[12 - FR - Part & Data Management]], [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]
SQL Server / ODBC databases (deployable as attached MDF) Store all technology/master data, the system rule-set, order/stock inventory, multilingual text and the DATADICT schema; accessed via ODBC and the MMK mini_sql binding. ITM / central tool management (via SW06) Live tool-availability and connection-state queries through the SW06 business-model/service-proxy .NET wrapper for networked tool magazines. [[19 - FR - Tools, Machines, Materials & Technology]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]

2.3 CAD-exchange partners & conversion tools

System Interaction Interface doc ref
External CAD systems (source of DXF, DWG, IGES, ME10/MI, DSTV, TAF) Provide customer geometry imported into the Dbase drawing model; TruTops exports DXF/ME10/IGES/SVG/HPGL/GEO/XML/XCAD back to them. [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]
ToPsVectorize (external raster vectorization tool, MCS) Converts raster images (BMP/JPG/PNG/TIF/GIF) to GEO vector geometry; invoked as an external executable (path from TOPS_VECTORIZE_PATH). [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]]
DWGConverter (external DWG→DXF tool) Wibu-licensed helper producing a temporary DXF from binary DWG for the native importer. [[11 - FR - Import, Export & File Formats]]
SolidWorks tube add-in (partner tube-CAD) Partner design integration linked through TubeConnectionDll; exchanges tube design data / TEO objects. [[15 - FR - Tube & Profile Machining (ToPs 400)]]

2.4 Embedded engines, libraries & programmatic surfaces

System Interaction Interface doc ref
GSSL NestLib (third-party true-shape nesting engine) Wrapped by tc_nest; receives parts + sheets + parameters and returns nested layouts, enforcing TRUMPF calling conventions and NERR_* error codes. [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]
GeoAlgorithmsKernel / BooleanLibraryAdapter (.NET geometry kernel) Managed geometry kernel invoked from native code for boolean/hull/shape operations on contours. [[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]]
Embedded Python 2.5 interpreter (with Tk / ToPsGraphics) Executes parametric-variant expressions, menu-button scripts and the startup customization hook; exposes the ToPs application-services module. Ships in every edition. [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]], [[10 - FR - CAD Geometry & Drawing Editing]]
Xidl remote-factory component API (third-party / in-house .NET clients) Per-edition external API (Cad/Laser/Nest/Tube/Combi) connected by registration URI + plugin name; managed/native marshalling, GUI-thread message-queue trigger. [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]]
Headless interop DLLs (reproduction, laser shop-floor programming) Pure-C P/Invoke DLLs with strong-named .NET wrappers for batch re-nesting and headless convert/nest/GMT/NC-list generation. [[23 - FR - Automation, Scripting & Integration]], [[17 - FR - Nesting & Palletizing]]
GeoViewer host application (ActiveX/OCX embedding) Hosts the geoViewerPlugin.ocx control and streams parts over HTTP to display GEO/MI/IGES/DXF/DSTV/DWG and export SVG/BMP. [[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]], [[30 - External Interface Requirements]]

2.5 Output devices & platform services

System Interaction Interface doc ref
Printers & pen plotters (Windows GDI printers, HPGL/HPGL2 plotters) Receive rendered drawings/nests/setup plans with margins, scaling, orientation and pen/paper profiles. [[21 - FR - Visualization, Viewing & Plotting]]
Dongle / Wibu licensing subsystem Validates per-edition and per-format keys (DXF/DWG/IGES/MI/DSTV passwords) and licence state; gates feature exposure, with a service superuser bypass. [[30 - External Interface Requirements]], [[50 - Constraints & Assumptions]]
Windows / (legacy) HP-UX platform, registry & file system Host OS for the GRIT UI (Win32 GDI, historically X11), registry-based plugin discovery, and the shared/transfer paths for job, TAF/TMT, NC and plan files. [[30 - External Interface Requirements]], [[50 - Constraints & Assumptions]]

3. Stakeholder Concerns

Beyond the interacting actors above, the following stakeholders hold an interest in the system's qualities without necessarily operating it hands-on. This section maps who cares about what onto the quality attributes specified in [[40 - Non-Functional Requirements]]. It is the seed of the NFR document's rationale and traceability.

3.1 Stakeholders (interest holders)

  • Sheet-metal fabricator / shop owner — total cost of ownership, material yield, throughput, staff productivity.
  • Machining end customer — part accuracy and quality, on-time delivery (indirect, via the fabricator).
  • TRUMPF product management & R&D — maintainability, extensibility, edition licensing, backward compatibility of file/DB formats.
  • TRUMPF service & support — diagnosability, portability, upgrade/migration safety.
  • Machine safety / manufacturing engineering — collision-free motion, respect for machine limits, safe part removal.
  • IT / data governance (customer) — data integrity, access control, database and PDM reliability, licensing compliance.
  • Regulatory / standards — measurement-system correctness (metric/inch), localisation for international deployment.

3.2 Concern → quality-attribute map

Quality attribute (→ [[40 - Non-Functional Requirements]]) Stakeholders / actors who care most Why it matters here
Usability & interaction responsiveness CAD Designer, Machine Operator, Nesting Operator, shop-floor operator Dense GRIT dialogs, interactive picking, rubber-band echo and touch-panel viewing must feel immediate.
Performance & throughput Production Planner, Nesting Operator, CAM Programmer, shop owner Nesting utilization, cycle-time estimation, motion optimization and NC-generation speed drive machine productivity and material cost.
Correctness & geometric accuracy CAM Programmer, CAD Designer, Machine Operator, end customer Geometry kernel results, bend-deduction factors, kerf/corner correction and NC output must be dimensionally correct.
Safety (collision avoidance & machine limits) Machine Operator, CAM Programmer, manufacturing engineering Collision-free path finding, clamp/dead-zone avoidance and axis-dynamics limits prevent machine and part damage.
Interoperability & format fidelity CAM Programmer, System Integrator, external CAD partners DXF/DWG/IGES/MI/DSTV import and multi-format export must preserve geometry and technology intent.
Reliability & data integrity System/DB Administrator, Production Planner, IT governance Versioned geometry files, DATADICT-defined schema, referential integrity, record locking and PDM check-in/out must not corrupt or lose data.
Maintainability & configurability Technology & Rules Administrator, Tool Administrator, UI/Mask Developer, TRUMPF R&D Rulesets, technology tables, tool catalogs, .org/.ini config and .gritx masks are the primary adaptation surface.
Security, access control & licensing System/DB Administrator, TRUMPF Service, IT governance Dongle/Wibu + password licensing, DB access roles and superuser bypass govern who may do what.
Portability & platform independence System/DB Administrator, System Integrator, TRUMPF Service Windows (and legacy HP-UX/X11) support, SQL Server / attached-MDF duality, and metric↔inch database duality.
Localisation & internationalisation System Integrator, global operators, standards stakeholders Up to 30 UI languages (incl. CJK), per-language fonts, and metric/imperial unit rendering.
Automatability & integration Automation / Integration Engineer, Production Planner, TruTops Fab Embedded Python, ASM/MMK scripting, the Xidl API, the Fab/MES adapter and headless DLLs enable batch and MES-integrated operation.
Compatibility & upgradability TRUMPF Service, System/DB Administrator, TRUMPF R&D Versioned .ppj, MI/GEO and DB schemas must migrate forward without breaking existing installations.
